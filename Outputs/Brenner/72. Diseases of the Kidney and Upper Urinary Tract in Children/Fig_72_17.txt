Fig. 72.17 Mean spontaneous change in estimated glomerular filtration rate (GFR) in 385 children with chronic kidney disease (CKD) randomly assigned to treatment conditions in the ESCAPE trial (measured during the run-in period). The rate of renal failure progression in children depends on underlying renal disease, age, and CKD stage. (Three bar charts showing the annualized loss of GFR. The first chart shows that glomerulopathies lead to a greater GFR loss than renal hypo/dysplasia. The second chart shows GFR loss increases with age, peaking in late adolescence. The third chart shows GFR loss accelerates as CKD stage progresses from 2 to 4.)
