Fig. 38.22 Comparison of net acid excretion between normal subjects and uric acid (UA) stone formers. Studies were performed with a controlled metabolic diet. Despite equivalent amounts of exogenous acid intake (same urinary sulfate excretion, not shown), uric acid stone formers were found to have higher net acid excretion, and a lower fraction of the net acid was carried by ammonium. TA, titratable acid. (Adapted from data from references 516, 523, and 524.)
