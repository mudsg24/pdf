Fig. 38.16 Comparison of the wild-type versus the Slc26a6 mouse. Slc26a6 mediates intestinal oxalate secretion in wild-type mice, so that only 10% of ingested oxalate is normally absorbed and excreted into the urine. Targeted deletion of the Slc26a6 gene in mice unmasks a large intestinal absorptive flux of oxalate and leads to increased plasma oxalate levels, hyperoxaluria, and calcium oxalate stones.
