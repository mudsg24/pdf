Fig. 25.60 Autosomal dominant polycystic kidney disease: computed tomographic scan. Noncontrast (A), nephrographic phase (B), and excretory phase (C) axial images. The kidneys are bilaterally enlarged, and the multiple various-sized cysts involve both kidneys. The calyces are splayed apart and appear distorted in the excretory phase image (C). Note the multiple small cysts also present in the involved liver.
