Fig. 25.79 Transitional cell carcinoma. Coronal (A) and axial (B) T2-weighted images show intermediate signal intensity and an infiltrating mass (arrow) within the atrophic lower pole moiety of a duplicated left kidney. Coronal (C) and axial (D) gadolinium-enhanced T1-weighted images show enhancing material within dilated calyces and pelvis of the lower pole moiety (arrow). The cortical atrophy is well demonstrated.
