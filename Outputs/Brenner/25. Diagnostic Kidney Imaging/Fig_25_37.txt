Fig. 25.37 Ureteral stone: noncontrast computed tomographic scan. Axial images of the kidneys show perinephric and peripelvic stranding and fluid on the right (A) caused by forniceal rupture and leakage of urine as a result of the distal obstructing stone at the right ureterovesical junction (B). Note the phlebolith on the right posterior to the bladder and lateral to the seminal vesicle; phleboliths are commonly confused with distal ureteral stones.
