Fig. 25.40 Subcapsular hematoma after lithotripsy. Coronal T2-weighted sequence (A) demonstrates high-signal intensity blood contained by left renal capsule (arrowheads). Axial T1-weighted image (B) and gadolinium-enhanced T1-weighted image (C) show mass effect on left kidney (arrowheads) caused by a subcapsular hematoma. The signal intensity is consistent with the presence of intracellular methemoglobin.
