Fig. 45.7 Autosomal dominant polycystic kidney disease seen in a parasagittal or longitudinal sonogram. This view of the right kidney was obtained with the patient in the right anterior oblique position. The approximate outline of the kidney is indicated by the broken line. Some of the larger renal cysts are indicated by Cs. The liver (L) is at the top of the figure. The right dome of the diaphragm (D) is at the lower left.
