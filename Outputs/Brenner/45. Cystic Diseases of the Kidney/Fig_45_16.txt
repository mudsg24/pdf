Fig. 45.16 Computed tomography (CT) scan of autosomal recessive polycystic kidney disease in an 18-year-old man whose serum creatinine and liver function test results were within normal ranges. The patient had clinical evidence of portal hypertension (gastric varices and enlarged spleen). An oral contrast agent was given to highlight the intestines. (A) CT scan without contrast enhancement. The liver is enlarged but not cystic. The kidneys are slightly enlarged and contain focal radiodense areas (nephrolithiasis). (B) CT scan after intravenous administration of iodinated radiocontrast agent showing cystic areas in both kidneys. The renal calcifications are now obscured by contrast medium in the collecting systems.
