Fig. 53.3 Calcium-sensing receptor (CaR). Activation of the CaR by calcium stimulates phospholipase C, leading to increased inositol 1,4,5-triphosphate (IP3), which mobilizes intracellular calcium and inhibits parathyroid hormone (PTH) synthesis. A decrease in serum calcium (Ca2+) inhibits intracellular signaling, leading to increased PTH synthesis and secretion. ER, Endoplasmic reticulum. (From Friedman PA, Goodman WG. PTH(1-84)/PTH(7-84): a balance of power. Am J Physiol Renal Physiol. 2006;290:F975-F984.)
