Fig. 30.12 The "overfill" mechanism of edema formation. The abnormal renal sodium retention is the consequence of blunted natriuretic response to atrial natriuretic peptide, increased epithelial sodium channel (ENaC) activity, and Na+-K+-ATPase activity, and is the key event of the process. The resulting hypervolemia alters Starling forces across the capillary wall at the local tissue level, leading to overflow edema. cGMP, Cyclic guanosine monophosphate; RAAS, renin-angiotensin aldosterone system.
