Fig. 70.5 Algorithm for management of allograft dysfunction in early posttransplant period. A flowchart for managing elevated creatinine. First, exclude/treat volume depletion, TMA, and pyelonephritis, and use imaging to rule out urological/vascular causes. Then, check cyclosporine/tacrolimus trough blood concentration. If CNI levels are high, reduce the dose and re-evaluate in 2-4 days. If no improvement, proceed to biopsy. If CNI levels are not elevated, proceed to biopsy. If biopsy is high risk/unavailable, an empiric steroid boost can be given. If there's no response in 3-5 days, a biopsy is recommended. The biopsy leads to specific intervention. CNI, Calcineurin inhibitor.
