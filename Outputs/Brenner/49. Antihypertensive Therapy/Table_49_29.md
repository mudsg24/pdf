| Table 49.29 Considerations for Initial Therapy in Older Patients Clinical Observation  Pharmacologic Considerations    Decreased vascular compliance and peripheral vascular resistance  Use vasodilator (e.g., HCTZ, ACE inhibitor, ARB, CCB,  \alpha -blocker).    Isolated systolic hypertension and wide pulse pressure  Use vasodilator (e.g., HCTZ, CCB).    Reduction of cardiovascular baroreflex function with blood pressure lability  Avoid sympatholytics and volume depletion. Use  \beta -blockers cautiously.    Orthostatic hypertension during recumbency  Consider using short-acting medications (<8-h duration) at bedtime.    Reduced metabolic capability  Adjust all medications for renal and hepatic function; start at half-dose.    Prostatic hypertrophy >20 mm Hg from systolic goal  Use  \alpha -blocker.Use fixed-dose combination therapy (ACE inhibitor/ CCB, ARB/CCB, ACE inhibitor/HCTZ, ARB/ HCTZ,  \beta -blocker/HCTZ). ACE, Angiotensin-converting enzyme; ARB, angiotensin II receptor blocker; CCB, calcium channel blocker; HCTZ, thiazide diuretic. |
