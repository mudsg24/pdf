Fig. 6.23 K+ transport along the nephron. Approximately 90% of filtered K+ is reabsorbed by the proximal tubule and the loop of Henle. K+ is secreted along the initial and cortical collecting duct. Net reabsorption occurs in response to K+ depletion, primarily within the medullary collecting duct. ADH, Antidiuretic hormone; ALDO, aldosterone; CCD, cortical collecting duct; DCT, distal convoluted tubule; MCD, medullary collecting duct; PCT, proximal convoluted tubule; R, reabsorption; S, secretion; TAL, thick ascending limb.
