Fig. 39.18 Pathway landscape of diabetic nephropathy. Nodes of the graph represent KEGG and Panther pathways (node diameter scales with number of protein coding genes assigned), edges between nodes scale with the number of genes overlapping, as well as interactions of genes across pathways according to the protein interaction network. Pathways are marked for holding biomarker candidates (green) and drug target candidates (red). (From Heinzel A, Perco P, Mayer G, Oberbauer R, Lukas A, Mayer B. From molecular signatures to predictive biomarkers: modeling disease pathophysiology and drug mechanism of action. Front Cell Dev Biol. 2014;2:37.)
