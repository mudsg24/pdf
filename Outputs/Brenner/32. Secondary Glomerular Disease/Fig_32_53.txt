Fig. 32.53 Human immunodeficiency virus–associated nephropathy. The characteristic pattern of collapsing glomerular sclerosis is depicted. Glomerular capillary lumina are occluded by wrinkling and retraction of the glomerular capillary walls associated with marked hypertrophy and hyperplasia of the visceral epithelial cells, forming a pseudocrescent (periodic acid–Schiff, ×325).
