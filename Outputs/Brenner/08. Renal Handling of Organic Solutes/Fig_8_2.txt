Fig. 8.2 Tubular reabsorption and urinary excretion of glucose as a function of filtered glucose. Tubular reabsorption of glucose increases linearly with the filtered glucose load until reabsorption reaches the tubular reabsorption capacity (Tmax Glucose) and glucose starts appearing in the urine. Theoretically, a Tmax of ~350 mg/min and normal glomerular filtration rate would result in a plasma glucose threshold of ~15.5 mmol/L. However, due to variability of Tmax in individual nephrons, the plasma glucose concentration that results in glucosuria in a normal individual is ~10-11.1 mmol/L (see "Splay"). SGLT2 inhibition reduces the renal glucose reabsorption to the transport capacity of SGLT1 and shifts the function to the left-that is, it reduces the renal glucose threshold (~3 mM) and Tmax (~150 mg/min).
