Fig. 21.6 (A) Apical renal sodium transporter expression, quantified by immunoblotting, in 4-week-old rat offspring from maternal low-protein diet. (A) A bar chart shows the percent of control for various sodium transporters. NHE3 and ENaC-α, -β, and -γ are around 100%. BSC1 is significantly increased to ~300% (**P < .001**). TSC is significantly increased to ~150% (*P < .05*). (B) A bar chart shows the rate of chloride transport in the medullary thick ascending limb. Both prenatal low protein and prenatal dexamethasone groups show significantly increased transport compared to the control group (*P < .05*). (C) A bar chart shows net sodium excretion after administration of an ENaC inhibitor. The 6% protein group shows significantly higher sodium excretion compared to the 20% protein group (*P < .05*). (From Vehaskari VM, Woods LL. Prenatal programming of hypertension: lessons from experimental models. J Am Soc Nephrol. 2005;16:2545-2556.) (B) (From Dagan A, Habib S, Gattineni J, et al. Prenatal programming of rat thick ascending limb chloride transport by low-protein diet and dexamethasone. Am J Physiol Regul Integr Comp Physiol. 2009;297:R93-R99.) (C) (From Cheng CJ, Lozano G, Baum M. Prenatal programming of rat cortical collecting tubule sodium transport. Am J Physiol Renal Physiol. 2012;302:F674-F678.)
