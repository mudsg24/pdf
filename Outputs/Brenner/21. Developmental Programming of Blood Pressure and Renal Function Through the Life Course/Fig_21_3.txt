Fig. 21.3 Architecture of wild type (WT), FGF7 null mutant, vitamin A-deficient, and protein-deficient mouse kidneys. Embryonic day 15.5 ureteric tree tracings for each condition (to scale). N is the maximum branching generation number, and Glom represents the mean glomerular count (minimum of three kidneys studied per condition). In each case, glomerular count is reduced compared with the wild type. The number of branching generations is specific to each condition. *   **WT:** N=15, Glom 101 ± 6 *   **FGF7-/-:** N=12, Glom 71 ± 7 *   **Vit. A-deficient:** N=17, Glom 44 ± 5 *   **Protein-deficient:** N=13, Glom 25 ± 4 (From Sampogna RV, Schneider L, Al-Awqati Q. Developmental program- ming of branching morphogenesis in the kidney.
