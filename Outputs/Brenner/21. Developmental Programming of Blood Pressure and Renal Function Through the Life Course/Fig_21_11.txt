Fig. 21.11 Cumulative risk for end-stage renal disease (ESRD) in individuals with age, according to whether the individual was (A) low birth weight (LBW) or (B) small for gestational age (SGA). (A) A line graph shows the cumulative risk of ESRD over age. Individuals with LBW have a progressively higher cumulative risk of ESRD compared to individuals without LBW. (B) A similar line graph shows that individuals with SGA also have a progressively higher cumulative risk of ESRD compared to those without SGA. From Ruggajo P, Skrunes R, Svarstad E, et al. Low birth weight, and development of ESRD: a nationwide registry study. Am J Kidney Dis. 2016;67:601-608.
