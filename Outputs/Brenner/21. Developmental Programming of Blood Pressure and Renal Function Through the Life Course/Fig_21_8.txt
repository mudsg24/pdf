Fig. 21.8 Relationship between birth weight, preterm birth, and blood pressure. (A) A forest plot shows a meta-analysis of the odds for hypertension (HTN) in individuals with low birth weight (LBW) compared to normal birth weight. The overall pooled odds ratio is 1.21 (95% CI: 1.13, 1.30), indicating an increased risk of HTN with LBW. (B) A forest plot shows a meta-analysis of the difference in systolic blood pressure (SBP) between individuals born preterm or with very low birth weight (VLBW) compared with full term. The overall pooled SBP difference is 2.50 mmHg (95% CI: 1.67, 3.32), indicating higher SBP in the preterm/VLBW group. (From Mu M, Wang SF, Sheng J, et al. Birth weight and subsequent blood pressure: a meta-analysis. Arch Cardiovasc Dis. 2012;105:99-113; see original paper for full references). (B) (From de Jong F, Monuteaux MC, van Elburg RM, et al. Systematic review and metaanalysis of preterm birth and later systolic blood pressure. Hypertension. 2012;59:226-234; see original paper for full references).
