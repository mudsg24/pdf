Fig. 21.4 Effect of intrauterine grown restriction (IUGR) on nephron number in humans. (A) Two graphs are shown. The upper panel shows a positive correlation between nephron number at birth and gestational age. The lower panel shows that there is no change in nephron number with postnatal age, indicating a lack of postnatal catch-up. (B) Two scatterplots are shown. The upper panel shows a positive correlation between birth weight and the number of glomeruli. The lower panel shows an inverse correlation between the number of glomeruli and glomerular volume in neonates. (From Hinchliffe SA, Lynch MR, Sargent PH, et al. The effect of intrauterine growth retardation on the development of renal nephrons. Br J Obstet Gynaecol. 1992;99:296-301.) (B) (From Manalich R, Reyes L, Herrera M, et al. Relationship between weight at birth and the number and size of renal glomeruli in humans: a histomorphometric study. Kidney Int. 2000;58:770-773.)
