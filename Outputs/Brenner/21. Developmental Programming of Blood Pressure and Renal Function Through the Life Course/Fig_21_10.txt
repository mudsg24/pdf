Fig. 21.10 Correlation between birth weight and salt sensitivity in 27 normotensive adults. A scatter plot shows an inverse correlation between birth weight (kg) and salt sensitivity (mm Hg). As birth weight increases, salt sensitivity of blood pressure decreases (R = −0.06; P = .002). (From de Boer MP, Ijzerman RP, de Jongh RT, et al. Birth weight relates to salt sensitivity of blood pressure in healthy adults. Hypertension 51:928-932, 2008.)
