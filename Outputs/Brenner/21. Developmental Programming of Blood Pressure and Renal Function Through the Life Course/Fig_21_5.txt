Fig. 21.5 Relationship between nephron number and mass of the right kidney in Caucasian infants aged ≤3 months who died within the first 3 months of life. A scatter plot shows a positive linear correlation between kidney weight (g) on the x-axis and the number of glomeruli per kidney (Nglom/1000) on the y-axis. As kidney weight increases, the number of glomeruli also increases. (From Zhang Z, Quinlan J, Hoy WE, et al. A common RET variant is associated with reduced newborn kidney size and function. J Am Soc Nephrol. 2008;19:2027-2034.)
