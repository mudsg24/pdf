Fig. 15.14 Plasma arginine vasopressin (AVP) responses to arterial hypotension produced by the infusion of trimethaphan in patients with central DI (cranial diabetes insipidus) and osmoreceptor dysfunction (adipsic diabetes insipidus). Normal responses in healthy volunteers are shown by the shaded area. Note that despite absent or markedly blunted AVP responses to hyperosmolality, patients with osmoreceptor dysfunction respond normally to baroreceptor stimulation induced by hypotension. (From Baylis PH, Thompson CJ: Diabetes insipidus and hyperosmolar syndromes. In Becker KL, ed. Principles and Practice of Endocrinology and Metabolism, Philadelphia: JB Lippincott; 1995:257.)
