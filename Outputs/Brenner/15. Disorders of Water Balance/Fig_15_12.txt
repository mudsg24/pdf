Fig. 15.12 Relationship between plasma arginine vasopressin (AVP) and concurrent plasma osmolality in patients with polyuria of diverse causes. All measurements were made at the end of a standard dehydration test. Shaded area, Range of normal. In patients with severe (◆) or partial (A) central diabetes insipidus (DI), plasma AVP was almost always subnormal relative to plasma osmolality. In contrast, the values from patients with primary polydipsia (O) or nephrogenic DI () were consistently within or above the normal range. (From Robertson GL: Diagnosis of diabetes insipidus. In Czernichow AP, Robinson A, eds. Diabetes Insipidus in Man: Frontiers of Hormone Research. Basel, Switzerland: S. Karger; 1985:176.)
