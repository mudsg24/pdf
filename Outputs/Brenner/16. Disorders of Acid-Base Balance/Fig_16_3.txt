Fig. 16.3 Synchrony of regulation of ammonium production (from glutamine [GLN] precursors, and excretion). Process allows generation of "new" HCO3 by the kidney. NH4+ excretion is regulated in response to changes in systemic acid-base and K+ balance. Contributing segments include the proximal convoluted tubule, proximal straight tubule, thin descending limb, thick ascending limb, and medullary collecting duct. Upregulated by acidosis and hypokalemia. Inhibited by hyperkalemia.
