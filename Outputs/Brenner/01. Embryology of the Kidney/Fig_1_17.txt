Fig. 1.17 Gradual recruitment of progenitor cells during nephrogenesis. Early recruited nephron progenitors clustering around the ureteric bud give rise to pretubular aggregates. New progenitors continue to be recruited and incorporate to the proximal end of the renal vesicle. Distal, medial, and proximal domains are established by the S-shaped body stage. NPC, Nephron progenitor cell; LOH, loop of Henle; RC, renal corpuscle. Adapted from Lindström NO, De Sena Brandine G, Tran T, et al. Progressive recruitment of mesenchymal progenitors reveals a time-dependent process of cell fate acquisition in mouse and human nephrogenesis. Dev Cell 2018;45:651-660.
