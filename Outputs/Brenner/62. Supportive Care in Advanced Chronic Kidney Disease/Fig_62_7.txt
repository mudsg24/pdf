Fig. 62.7 Conceptualizing palliative dialysis. This diagram shows two overlapping circles. The larger circle is "PATIENT-CENTERED DIALYSIS," with bullet points: Align treatment with patient preferences, Survival and long-term health outcomes are balanced with optimizing QOL and symptom control, and Requires the integration of kidney supportive care. The smaller, overlapping circle is "PALLIATIVE DIALYSIS," with bullet points: Align treatment with patient preferences, Kidney supportive care to optimize QOL, symptom control, and advance care planning for end-of-life care become of paramount importance. QOL, Quality of life. (Adapted from Davison SN, Jassal SV. Supportive care: integration of patient-centered kidney care to manage symptoms and geriatric syndromes. Clin J Am Soc Nephrol. 2016;11:1882-1891.)
