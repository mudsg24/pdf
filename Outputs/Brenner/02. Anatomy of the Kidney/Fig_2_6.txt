Fig. 2.6 Light micrograph of a sagittal section of normal mouse kidney. The renal cortex is the region between the arcuate vessels and the renal capsule. The borders between the outer stripe of the outer medulla (OMo), inner stripe or the outer medulla (OMi), and inner medulla (IM) are easily distinguished by the changes in the staining intensity. The OMo, which contains proximal tubules, has a similar staining intensity as the cortex. OMi by comparison, has paler staining due to the absence of proximal tubules. IM stains even more weakly due to the absence of thick ascending limbs in this region. The tip of the papilla, identified by the area cribrosa, extends into the proximal ureter in mouse kidneys. Hematoxylin and eosin stain.
