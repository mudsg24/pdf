Fig. 2.51 Scanning electron micrograph of the terminal portion of rabbit inner medullary collecting duct. The cells are tall and covered with small microvilli on their luminal surfaces, but lack cilia. Small lateral cell processes project into the lateral intercellular spaces. (Modified from Madsen KM, Clapp WL, Verlander JW. Structure and function of the inner medullary collecting duct. Kidney Int. 1988;34:441.)
