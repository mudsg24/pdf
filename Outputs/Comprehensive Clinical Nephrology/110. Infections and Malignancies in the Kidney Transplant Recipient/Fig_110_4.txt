Fig. 110.4 Tissue Obtained From Patient’s Vomitus. Histologic examination of the necrotic tissue showed numerous fungal organisms (consistent with Candida spp.), and degenerated squamous epithelium with detached columnar (gastric foveolar type) epithelium. Occasional cells demonstrated enlarged nuclei and intracytoplasmic granular eosinophilic inclusions, consistent with cytomegalovirus viral inclusions.
