Fig. 35.9 Relative Risk for Stroke and Coronary Heart Disease Increases With Increased Diastolic Blood Pressure. (A) Cerebrovascular accident (stroke) data are from seven prospective observational studies and 843 events. (B) Coronary heart disease data are from nine studies and 4856 events. Size of squares is proportional to the number of events in each category; vertical lines indicate 95% confidence intervals. (Modified from Klag MJ, Whelton PK, Randall BL, et al. Blood pressure and end-stage renal disease in men. N Engl J Med. 1996;334[1]:13–18.)
