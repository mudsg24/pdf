Fig. 35.4 Pathogenesis of Primary Hypertension. Initiating factors induce renal ischemia and oxidative stress with the stimulation of innate immune reactivity, neoantigen formation, and autoimmune reactivity that drives inflammation in the kidney, in the adventitia in the arteries, and in the central nervous system that stimulates the sympathetic nervous system. Sodium retention results from increased reabsorption and impaired pressure natriuresis as a consequence of renal inflammation. Established hypertension drives target organ damage. Ang II, angiotensin II; CNS, central nervous system; DAMP, damage-associated molecular patterns; LVH, left ventricular hypertrophy; SNS, sympathetic nervous system.
