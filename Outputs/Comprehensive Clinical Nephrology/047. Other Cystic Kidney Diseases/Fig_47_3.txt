Fig. 47.3 Radiologic Findings Associated With Autosomal Recessive Polycystic Kidney Disease. (A) Autosomal recessive polycystic kidney disease (ARPKD) kidneys are enlarged and echogenic, with poor corticomedullary differentiation on ultrasound. (B) Delayed computed tomography image of kidneys in a patient with ARPKD shows persistent nephrogram with radiodense striations (arrowheads) and nonopaque, radially oriented cylindrical collections (arrows).
