Fig. 47.6 The TSC1/TSC2 complex regulates mTORC1 signaling by integrating extracellular and intracellular signals that promote metabolic homeostasis. Under conditions that promote cell growth, the hamartin-tuberin complex is inactivated by Akt, leading to Rheb activation and the phosphorylation of S6 kinase (S6K) and 4E-BP1 by mTORC1. Conversely, energy deprivation activates AMPK, which phosphorylates tuberin, activating the hamartin-tuberin complex to inactivate Rheb and mTORC1, thereby inhibiting cell growth. mTORC1 inhibitors act by binding to FKBP12, which interacts with mTORC1 to inhibit downstream signaling. (Modified from Inoki K, Corradetti MN, Guan KL. Dysregulation of the TSC-mTOR pathway in human disease. Nat Genet. 2005;37(1):19–24.)
