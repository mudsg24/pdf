Fig. 47.8 Kidney Pathology in Tuberous Sclerosis Complex (TSC) (A) Cut section shows multiple kidney angiomyolipomas. (B) Light microscopy shows angiomyolipoma containing adipose tissue and spindle smooth muscle–like cells interspersed between abnormal vessels with thickened walls (H&E stain; ×16). (C) Light microscopy shows TSC cysts lined with distinctive epithelia consisting of large, acidophilic cells with hyperchromatic nuclei (H&E stain; ×65).
