Fig. 48.2 Immunohistochemistry of Glomerular Basement Membrane (GBM) in X-Linked Alport Syndrome. In a normal kidney, both α1/α2(IV) (A) and α5(IV) (B) are present in the GBM. In a male with X-linked Alport syndrome, α1/α2(IV) is present in the GBM (C), but α5(IV) is absent (D). In a female with X-linked Alport syndrome, α1/α2(IV) is present in the GBM (E), but α5(IV) shows a mosaic distribution (F), with alternating segments of α5-positive and α5-negative GBM.
