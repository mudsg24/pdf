## Figures

### Fig_48_1

![Fig_48_1](Fig_48_1.png)

Fig. 48.1 Genomic Organization of Type IV Collagen Genes. Each type IV collagen chain is synthesized from one of six distinct genes, which are arranged in head-to-head pairs on three different chromosomes. The COL4A5 and COL4A6 genes are on the X chromosome, the COL4A3 and COL4A4 genes are on chromosome 2, and the COL4A1 and COL4A2 genes are on chromosome 13.

### Fig_48_2

![Fig_48_2](Fig_48_2.png)

Fig. 48.2 Immunohistochemistry of Glomerular Basement Membrane (GBM) in X-Linked Alport Syndrome. In a normal kidney, both α1/α2(IV) (A) and α5(IV) (B) are present in the GBM. In a male with X-linked Alport syndrome, α1/α2(IV) is present in the GBM (C), but α5(IV) is absent (D). In a female with X-linked Alport syndrome, α1/α2(IV) is present in the GBM (E), but α5(IV) shows a mosaic distribution (F), with alternating segments of α5-positive and α5-negative GBM.

### Fig_48_3

![Fig_48_3](Fig_48_3.png)

Fig. 48.3 Immunohistochemistry of Epidermal Basement Membrane (EBM) in X-Linked Alport Syndrome. (A) In a normal individual, α1/α2(IV) (left) and α5(IV) (right) are both present in the EBM. (B) In a male with X-linked Alport syndrome (XLAS), α1/α2(IV) is present in the EBM (left), but α5(IV) is absent (right). (C) In a female with XLAS, α1/α2(IV) is present in the EBM (left), and α5(IV) is present in a mosaic distribution (right), with intermittent expression along the EBM.

### Fig_48_4

![Fig_48_4](Fig_48_4.png)

Fig. 48.4 Immunohistochemistry of Kidney in a Patient With Autosomal Recessive Alport Syndrome. (A) Normal glomerulus with expression of α5(IV) in both glomerular basement membrane (GBM) and Bowman capsule. (B) Kidney from a patient with autosomal recessive Alport syndrome (ARAS), with absence of α5(IV) from the GBM, but persistence in the basement membrane of Bowman capsule (arrow) and collecting ducts. (C) Normal skin showing expression of α5(IV) in epidermal basement membrane (EBM). (D) Skin from an ARAS patient showing normal expression of α5(IV) in the EBM.
