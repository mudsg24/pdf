Fig. 48.1 Genomic Organization of Type IV Collagen Genes. Each type IV collagen chain is synthesized from one of six distinct genes, which are arranged in head-to-head pairs on three different chromosomes. The COL4A5 and COL4A6 genes are on the X chromosome, the COL4A3 and COL4A4 genes are on chromosome 2, and the COL4A1 and COL4A2 genes are on chromosome 13.
