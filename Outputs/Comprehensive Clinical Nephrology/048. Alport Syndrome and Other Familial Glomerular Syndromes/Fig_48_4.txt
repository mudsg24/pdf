Fig. 48.4 Immunohistochemistry of Kidney in a Patient With Autosomal Recessive Alport Syndrome. (A) Normal glomerulus with expression of α5(IV) in both glomerular basement membrane (GBM) and Bowman capsule. (B) Kidney from a patient with autosomal recessive Alport syndrome (ARAS), with absence of α5(IV) from the GBM, but persistence in the basement membrane of Bowman capsule (arrow) and collecting ducts. (C) Normal skin showing expression of α5(IV) in epidermal basement membrane (EBM). (D) Skin from an ARAS patient showing normal expression of α5(IV) in the EBM.
