Fig. 48.3 Immunohistochemistry of Epidermal Basement Membrane (EBM) in X-Linked Alport Syndrome. (A) In a normal individual, α1/α2(IV) (left) and α5(IV) (right) are both present in the EBM. (B) In a male with X-linked Alport syndrome (XLAS), α1/α2(IV) is present in the EBM (left), but α5(IV) is absent (right). (C) In a female with XLAS, α1/α2(IV) is present in the EBM (left), and α5(IV) is present in a mosaic distribution (right), with intermittent expression along the EBM.
