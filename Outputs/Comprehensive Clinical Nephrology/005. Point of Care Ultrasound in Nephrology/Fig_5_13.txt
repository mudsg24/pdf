Fig. 5.13 Proposed Approach to Acute Dyspnea Using Lung Ultrasound Based on the Bedside Lung Ultrasound in Emergency (BLUE) Protocol. ^{4} Three zones on each side are assessed: the upper point (1), the lower point (2), and the posterolateral point (3).
