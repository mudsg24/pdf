**Usual Wait Time of 2 Years**
* Most cancers

**No Wait Time Necessary**
* Asymptomatic T1a (<4 cm) renal cell carcinoma without metastases or suspicious histologic features
* In situ carcinoma
* Focal neoplasm (defined as a localized tumor without metastases)
* Low-grade bladder cancer
* Basal cell skin cancer

**Wait Time of >2 Years May Be Necessary**
* Melanoma
* Breast cancer
* Colorectal cancer
* Uterine cancer
