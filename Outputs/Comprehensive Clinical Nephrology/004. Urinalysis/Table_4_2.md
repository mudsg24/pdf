Main causes of false-negative and false-positive results of urine reagent strips. False results also may occur when time-expired strips are used.

| Constituent | False-Negative Results | False-Positive Results |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Specific gravity (SG) | Urine pH > 6.5 | Urine protein > 7.0 g/L |
| pH | Reduced values in presence of formaldehyde | |
| Hemoglobin | High urine specific gravity<br>Ascorbic acid<br>Formaldehyde (0.5 g/L) used to preserve samples | Myoglobin<br>Microbial peroxidases |
| Glucose | Ascorbic acid<br>Bacteria | Very acidic urine pH<br>Oxidizing detergents |
| Albumin | Low urine specific gravity<br>Albumin < 0.25-0.30 g/L<br>Low urine<br>Tubular proteins<br>Monoclonal heavy/light chains | Urine SG ≥ 1.030<br>Urine pH > 8.0<br>Quaternary ammonium detergents<br>Chlorhexidine<br>Polyvinylpyrrolidone |
| Leukocyte esterase | High urine specific gravity<br>Ascorbic acid<br>Glucose ≥ 20 g/L<br>Protein >5.0 g/L<br>Cephalothin (strong inhibition)<br>Tetracycline (strong inhibition)<br>Cephalexin (moderate inhibition)<br>Tobramycin (mild inhibition) | Formaldehyde (0.4 g/L)<br>Imipenem<br>Meropenem<br>Clavulanate<br>Abnormally colored urine |
| Nitrites | Bacteria that do not reduce nitrates to nitrites<br>Lack of vegetables in diet<br>Short bladder incubation time | Abnormally colored urine |
| Ketones | Improper storage | Free sulfhydryl groups (e.g., captopril)<br>Levodopa<br>Abnormally colored urine |
