AKI, Acute kidney injury; ATN, acute tubular necrosis; GN, glomerulonephritis; RTEC, renal tubular epithelial cell.

| Kidney Disease | Hallmark | Associated Findings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| AKI with ATN (proteinuria: absent to +) | RTECS<br>Epithelial casts<br>Granular casts | Variable according to cause of ATN (e.g., high number of dysmorphic erythrocytes in active proliferative GN; myoglobin casts in rhabdomyolysis; uric acid crystals in acute urate nephropathy) |
| Acute interstitial nephritis (proteinuria: absent to +) | Leukocytes<br>Isomorphic erythrocytes | RTECS<br>Leukocyte casts<br>Erythrocyte casts |
| Active proliferative glomerulonephritis (proteinuria: + to ++++) | Dysmorphic erythrocytes (moderate to high number)<br>Erythrocyte/hemoglobin casts | Leukocytes (low to moderate number)<br>RTECS<br>Epithelial, hyaline, hyaline-granular, granular, waxy, leukocytic casts |
| Nephrotic syndrome (proteinuria: ++++) | Fat particles<br>High number of casts (fat, hyaline, hyaline-granular, granular epithelial) | Renal tubular epithelial cells<br>Dysmorphic erythrocytes (absent or low-to-moderate number) |
| Urinary tract infection (proteinuria: absent) | Bacteria<br>Leukocytes | Isomorphic erythrocytes<br>Transitional epithelial cells<br>Triple phosphate crystals (for infections caused by urease-producing bacteria)<br>Epithelial + leukocyte casts (in pyelonephritis) |
| Polyomavirus BK infection (proteinuria: absent) | Decoy cells | Decoy cell casts (in BK virus nephropathy)<br>Macrophages |
| Urologic diseases (proteinuria: absent) | Isomorphic erythrocytes (low to high number)<br>Leukocytes<br>Transitional cells (deep, superficial, atypical) | Crystals (urolithiasis)<br>Atypical/malignant transitional cell (neoplasia of excretory urinary system) |
