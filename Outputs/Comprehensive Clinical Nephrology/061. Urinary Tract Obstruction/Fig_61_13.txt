Fig. 61.13 Pathology of Chronic Ureteral Obstruction. Section of the rim of kidney tissue from the kidney shown in Fig. 61.7. The kidney capsule is at the top, the urinary space at the bottom. The cortex is considerably thinned, and only a few atrophic tubules remain (arrows) within an interstitium comprising dense fibrous tissue and a mononuclear cell infiltrate (blue staining nuclei). No glomeruli can be seen. This demonstrates why there would be no prospect for any significant functional recovery in this kidney even after the relief of the obstruction.
