Fig. 61.9 Computed Tomography Scan of the Abdomen Showing a Grossly Hydronephrotic Kidney on the Left. Arrows mark dilated kidney pelvis. Dilated loops of small bowel are seen in the right hypochondrium. Sequential sections demonstrated that the ureter was dilated along its length and that there was a pelvic mass, which was responsible for both bowel and left ureteral obstruction. The mass was subsequently shown to be arising from a carcinoma of the colon.
