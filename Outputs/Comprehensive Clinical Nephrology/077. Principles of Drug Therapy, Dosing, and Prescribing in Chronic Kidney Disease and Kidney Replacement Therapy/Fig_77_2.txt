Fig. 77.2 Fraction of Drug or Metabolite Excreted in Urine. In this hypothetical example, 10% of the dose is excreted unchanged in urine (fe = 10%); 50% of the dose is metabolized to inactive metabolite M1, which is then all renally excreted; 30% of the dose is metabolized to inactive metabolite M2, which is all renally excreted; and the remaining 10% is excreted unchanged in bile. For this drug, the total excretion of drug dose in urine is 90%. However, this 90% includes 10% as parent drug and 80% as inactive metabolites, and dose modification is probably not essential even in severe chronic kidney disease. Total kidney excretion of the dose is 90%; however, the clinically significant fraction of active drug excreted in urine is 10%.
