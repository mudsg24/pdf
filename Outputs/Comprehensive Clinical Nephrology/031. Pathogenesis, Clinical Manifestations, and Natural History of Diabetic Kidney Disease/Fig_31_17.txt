Fig. 31.17 Circadian Variation of Urinary Albumin Exretion (UAE). UAE is lower in resting conditions at night (left) than during daytime activity (right). Relationship between UAE assessed on two different days 1 week apart in patients with type 1 diabetes (open circles) and primary hypertension (closed circles). There is substantial individual day-to-day variation of albumin excretion and also between day and night collections. (From Redon J. Measurement of microalbuminuria: what the nephrologist should know. Nephrol Dial Transplant. 2006;21:573–576.)
