Fig. 52.2 Kidney Dysplasia. (A) Intravenous urogram (IVU) shows gross bilateral scarring in a 20-year-old woman who has been followed since the age of 2 years. Progressive scarring has been observed in the absence of urinary tract infections and obstruction. This probably represents primary kidney dysplasia. (B) Computed tomography IVU shows gross scarring of right kidney. (Courtesy Dr. A. Kirkham, University College Hospitals, London.)
