Fig. 52.8 Duplex Kidney. (A) Intravenous urogram shows a duplex left kidney. The lower pole is scarred and shows evidence of reflux damage. The two ureters enter the bladder separately, with the lower pole ureter in the abnormal location. The right kidney also shows features of reflux, with clubbing of the calyces and some scarring. (B) Computed tomography scan shows an isolated right-sided megaureter (arrows).
