Fig. 66.1 Mesoamerican Nephropathy. Major areas where Mesoamerican nephropathy has been observed are along the Pacific Coast, especially involving the Guanacaste region of Costa Rica, the Bajo Lempa region of El Salvador, and the Chinandega region of Nicaragua. These are also the hottest areas in Central America. (From Glaser J, Lemery J, Rajagopalan B, et al. Climate change and the emergent epidemic of chronic kidney disease from heat stress in rural communities: the case for heat stress nephropathy. Clin J Am Soc Nephrol. 2016;11[8]:1472–1483.)
