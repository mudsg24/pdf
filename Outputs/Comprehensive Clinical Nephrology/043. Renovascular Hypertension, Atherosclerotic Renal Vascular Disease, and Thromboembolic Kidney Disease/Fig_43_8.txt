Fig. 43.8 Magnetic Resonance Angiogram With and Without Gadolinium Contrast Enhancement. (A) High-grade stenosis affecting the left inferior renal artery is evident, with functioning kidney tissue as reflected by gadolinium nephrogram (black arrow). Concerns about the role of gadolinium in the development of nephrogenic systemic fibrosis have greatly reduced the use of this contrast agent. (B) As a result, methods to image the vasculature without contrast material are being developed that produce excellent reconstructed images (white arrow).
