Fig. 43.10 Thrombosis of Renal Artery Complicating Renal Artery Stenting. Right and left renal artery stents. The left renal stent (arrow) is triangular, indicating crimping of the proximal portion, which in this patient was associated with thrombosis of the renal artery, seen here as no contrast entering the vessel. The right renal stent is patent.
