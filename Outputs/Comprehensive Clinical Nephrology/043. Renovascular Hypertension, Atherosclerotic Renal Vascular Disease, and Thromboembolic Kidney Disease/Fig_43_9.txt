Fig. 43.9 Algorithm for the Management of Renal Artery Stenosis. The intensity of imaging and revascularization depends on both the level of kidney function and the blood pressure (BP), in addition to the comorbid disease risks for the individual patient. The overall goal should focus on stable kidney function and BP levels. As with any other vascular disease, monitoring for disease progression and recurrence is an important element of long-term management. ACEI, Angiotensin-converting enzyme inhibitor; ARB, angiotensin receptor blocker; CTA, computed tomographic angiography; MRA, magnetic resonance angiography; RAAS, renin-angiotensin-aldosterone system.
