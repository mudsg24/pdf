Fig. 65.1 Renal Tuberculosis. Noncaseating granulomas with epithelioid cells in miliary tuberculosis (arrows show the peripheries of the granulomas). Although the typical pathologic change is granuloma with caseous necrosis with Langerhans-type giant cells, these atypical granulomas can be observed in tuberculosis and should be differentiated from sarcoidosis. (H&E stain.) (Courtesy Dr. Noriko Uesugi, Ibaraki, Japan.)
