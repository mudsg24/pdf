Fig. 11.3 Calcium Homeostasis in Healthy Adults. Net zero $Ca^{2+}$ balance is the result of net intestinal absorption (absorption minus secretion) and urinary excretion, which by definition are the same. After its passage into the extracellular fluid, $Ca^{2+}$ enters the extracellular space, is deposited in bone, or is eliminated via the kidneys. Entry and exit fluxes between the extracellular and intracellular spaces (skeletal and nonskeletal compartments) are also of identical magnitude under steady-state conditions.
