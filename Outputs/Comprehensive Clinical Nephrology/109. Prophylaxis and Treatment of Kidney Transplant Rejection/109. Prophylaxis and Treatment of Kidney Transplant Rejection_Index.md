### Fig_109_2

![Fig_109_2](Fig_109_2.png)

Fig. 109.2 Biopsy Technique. Acute rejection begins as patchy, focal infiltrates and becomes homogeneous only in advanced stages. The intensity of mononuclear infiltrate seen on the biopsy sample would differ between core 1 and core 2. Routinely taking two core biopsy samples can help decrease the sampling errors, which can affect the histologic interpretation of rejection.

Fig. 109.3 Acute Antibody-Mediated Rejection. (A) Peritubular and glomerular capillaries contain numer- ous polymorphonuclear leukocytes and mononuclear cells. (B) Numerous polymorphonuclear leukocytes are observed in a peritubular capillary (arrows). Interstitial edema is noted. (PAS stain, ×200.)
