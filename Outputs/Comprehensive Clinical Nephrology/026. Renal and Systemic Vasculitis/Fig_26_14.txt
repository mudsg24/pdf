Fig. 26.14 Kawasaki Disease Arteritis Affecting Renal Interlobar Artery in a Young Child. The artery wall is intact on the far left. The remainder of the wall has extensive edema, infiltration by mononuclear leukocytes, and a band of fuchsinophilic (red) fibrinoid material roughly at the junction between the inflamed intima and muscularis. (Masson trichrome, ×25.)
