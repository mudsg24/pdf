Fig. 63.6 Extraanatomic Stenting for Malignant Ureteral Obstruction. Plain radiograph showing placement of an extraanatomic stent for malignant obstruction of the right ureter. The upper end of the double-J stent has been placed in the right kidney pelvis (arrow). The stent then runs through a subcutaneous tunnel before the lower end enters the bladder (arrowhead).
