ESWL is the first choice for stone intervention, except in those circumstances that may favor PCNL. aStone composition can be defined with certainty only by direct stone analysis, but advances in imaging may ultimately provide a means to accurately assess stone composition in situ before treatment, thus allowing the urologist to select the treatment most likely to be successful. ESWL, Extracorporeal shock wave lithotripsy; PCNL, percutaneous nephrolithotomy; PUJ, pelviureteral junction.
