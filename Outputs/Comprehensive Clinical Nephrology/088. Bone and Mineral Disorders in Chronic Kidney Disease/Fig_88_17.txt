Fig. 88.17 β2-Microglobulin (Aβ2M) Amyloid Deposition in the Femoral Head. Postmortem specimen from a patient who had undergone long-term hemodialysis. Two large lesions (arrowheads), partly filled with grayish amyloid and partly cystic, are noted in the femoral head. Also note the marked thickening of the synovial capsule from amyloid deposition (arrow).
