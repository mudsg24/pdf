**Congenital Causes**
*   Autosomal dominant
*   Autosomal recessive

**Acquired Causes**
*   Posttraumatic
*   Iatrogenic (postsurgical)
*   Tumors (metastatic from breast, craniopharyngioma, pinealoma)
*   Histiocytosis
*   Granuloma (tuberculosis, sarcoid)
*   Aneurysm
*   Meningitis
*   Encephalitis
*   Guillain-Barré syndrome
*   Drugs
*   Idiopathic
*Entries in italics are the most common causes.*
