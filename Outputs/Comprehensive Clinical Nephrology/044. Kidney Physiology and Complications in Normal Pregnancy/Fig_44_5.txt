Fig. 44.5 Proteins Soluble fms-like Tyrosine Kinase 1 (sFlt-1) and Soluble Endoglin (sEng) Cause Endothelial Dysfunction in Preeclampsia by Neutralizing Circulating Vascular Endothelial Growth Factor (VEGF), Placental Growth Factor (PlGF), and Transforming Growth Factor-β (TGFβ). Loss of these proangiogenic factors leads to reduced synthesis of prostacyclin and nitric oxide and increased production of endothelin-1, with systemic vasoconstriction, vascular leak, and generalized endothelial damage.
