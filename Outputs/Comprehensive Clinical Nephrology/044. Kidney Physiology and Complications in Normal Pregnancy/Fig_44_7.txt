Fig. 44.7 Glomerular Endotheliosis. (A) Normal glomerulus on light microscopy. (B) Glomerulus from a woman with preeclampsia showing severe endotheliosis with swelling of endothelial cells resulting in marked capillary lumen occlusion and tuft enlargement. (C) High-power view demonstrating subendothelial fibrinoid deposits and swollen endothelial cells.
