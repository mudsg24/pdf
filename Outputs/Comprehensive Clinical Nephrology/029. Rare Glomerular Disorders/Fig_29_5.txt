Fig. 29.5 Antiphospholipid Syndrome. (A) Thrombosis of the peri- and intraglomerular arteriole and segmental glomerular capillaries (arrows) with associated mesangiolysis characterized by mesangial matrix dissolution (arrowhead). The adjacent glomerular capillaries show ischemic wrinkling and retraction. (B) Arteriole with luminal fibrin-platelet thrombus and endothelial cell loss. Note the muscular wall is intact and without inflammation.
