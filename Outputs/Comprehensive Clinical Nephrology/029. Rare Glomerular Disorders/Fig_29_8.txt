Fig. 29.8 Lecithin–Cholesterol Acyltransferase (LCAT) Deficiency. (A) Irregular, thickened and double contoured glomerular capillary walls containing clear vacuoles, which are characteristic of LCAT deficiency. (B) Electron microscopy reveals electron dense lipid material in electron lucent lacunae (arrows) within the glomerular basement membranes.
