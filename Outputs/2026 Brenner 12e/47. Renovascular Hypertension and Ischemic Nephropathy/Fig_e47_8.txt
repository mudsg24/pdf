Fig. e47.8 Probability of identifying renal artery stenosis based on clinical features. These data were derived from 477 patients in referra centers for treatment-resistant hypertension (HTN) in the Netherlands. Overall prevalence was 22.4%, illustrating that even in “enriched” patient populations, renovascular disease is not present in the majority. Clinical features allowed selection of patients for testing with a relatively high pretest probability of disease, which affects the validity of testing schemes. (From Krijnen P, van Jaarsveld BC, Steyerberg EW, et al. A clinica prediction rule for renal artery stenosis. Ann Intern Med. 1998;129:705–711.)
