Fig. 47.7 Computed tomography angiogram (A) of high-grade vascular occlusion leading to left kidney atrophy while the right kidney is wel perfused beyond an endovascular stent. T2 signals from magnetic resonance imaging (B) illustrate a gradient of perfusion from cortex to lower levels in the deeper medullary sections, especially in the left kidney. Mapping of the blood-oxygen-level-dependent (BOLD) magnetic resonance R2\* levels (a function of deoxyhemoglobin) (C and D) illustrates the normal gradient in the right kidney from cortex (low R2\*, blue) to deeper medullary segments that have progressively higher levels of R2\* (green to red). The R2\* map of the left kidney (D) illustrates overt cortical hypoxia evident beyond critical vascular occlusion associated with a larger fraction of the slice having high R2\* values (red) associated with fractiona medullary hypoxia.
