Fig. e47.11 (A and B) Isotope renography in a patient with unilateral renal artery stenosis. (A) A <sup>99m</sup>Tc-DTPA scan demonstrates delayed circulation and excretion of isotope on the left. (B) A hippuran scan (now replaced with <sup>99m</sup>Tc-mercaptoacetyltriglycine) provides a renogram demonstrating a small kidney with impaired renal function on the affected side. Radionuclide scans provide a comparative estimate of function from each kidney that may facilitate selection of intervention including the potential effect of nephrectomy. DTPA, Diethylenetriamine pentaacetic acid.
