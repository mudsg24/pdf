Fig. 47.8 (A and B) Angiographic appearance of medial fibroplasia with serial intravascular webs and small aneurysmal dilatations between them. These lesions appear in the midportion of the vessel, have a predilection for the right renal artery, and are most common in women. (B) As shown here, these lesions can often be improved substantially be balloon angioplasty.
