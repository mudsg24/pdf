Fig. 1.9 Generation of kidney organoid based on development. Representative-directed differentiation protocols of pluripotent stem cells (PSCs) toward nephron progenitor (NP), ureteric bud (UB), and stromal progenitor (SP) lineages are summarized. Induced progenitors are further differentiated to form nephron organoids (right upper panel) or ureteric bud organoids (right middle panel), or they are reassembled to reconsti- tute higher-order kidney structures (right lower panel). Commonly used signaling molecules among multiple reports are shown in bold. A factor specifically used in human PSC differentiation is shown in red. AIM, Anterior intermediate mesoderm; ECM, extracellular matrix; PIM, posterior intermediate mesoderm; PS, primitive streak; PSC, pluripotent stem cell; RA, retinoic acid; TBM, tail bud mesenchyme; WD, Wolffian duct.
