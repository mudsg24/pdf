Fig. 1.7 Cre-lox homologous gene recombination system. Sim- plified overview of Cre recombinase–mediated homologous recombi- nation for generation of tissue-specific conditional null mutations of a target gene. Cre recombinase expression is engineered to be driven under the control of tissue-specific promoter (promoter<sup>TS</sup>). The target gene (or typically certain exons within the target chromosomal locus) is flanked with loxP sites (recognition sites for Cre recombinase). Cre activity in specific cell types mediates the excision of the loxP-flanked (floxed) target gene, creating a null allele.
