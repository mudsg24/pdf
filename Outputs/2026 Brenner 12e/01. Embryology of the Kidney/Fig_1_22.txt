Fig. 1.22 In vitro reconstitution of higher-order kidney structures from pluripotent stem cells. “Higher-order” kidney structures are gener- ated in vitro when stromal progenitors (iSP), nephron progenitors (iNP), and a uteteric bud (iUB) derived from mouse embryonic stem cells (ESCs) are assembled and cultured at the air/liquid interface for 7 days. Note the multiply branched collecting ducts (KRT8) surrounded by nephron progenitors (SIX2) at the periphery and the formation of numerous glomeruli (NPHS1), proximal renal tubules (LTL) and distal tubules (CDH1). (Adapted with permission from Tanigawa S, Tanaka E, Miike K, et al. Generation of the organotypic kidney structure by integrating pluripotent stem cell-derived renal stroma. Nat Commun. 2022;13(1):611.)
