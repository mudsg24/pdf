Fig. 1.19 Development of cellular diversity in the collecting duct. Lateral inhibition in collecting duct differentiation. The combi- nation of Foxi1 and Tfcp2l1 induces the expression of intercalated (IC) genes including Jag1, which triggers Notch signaling in adja- cent principal cells. This results in the activation of Hes1 (Notch-ON) and the silencing of IC genes in the principal cells. Notch activity in Jag1-expressing ICs remains low (Notch-OFF). (Adapted with permis- sion from Werth M, Schmidt-Ott K, Leete T, et al. Transcription factor TFCP2L1 patterns cells in the mouse kidney collecting ducts. eLife. 2017;6:e24265.)
