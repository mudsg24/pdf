Fig. 1.16 Gradual recruitment of progenitor cells during nephrogenesis. Early recruited nephron progenitors clustering around the ureteric bud give rise to pretubular aggregates. New progenitors continue to be recruited and incorporate into the proximal end of the renal vesicle. Distal, medial, and proximal domains are established by the S-shaped body stage. LOH, Loop of Henle; NPC, nephron progenitor cell; RC, rena corpuscle. (Adapted from Lindström NO, De Sena Brandine G, Tran T, et al. Progressive recruitment of mesenchymal progenitors reveals a timedependent process of cell fate acquisition in mouse and human nephrogenesis. Dev Cell. 2018;45:651–660.)
