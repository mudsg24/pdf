Fig. 1.23 FGF-SHH-BMP4 signaling axis in early ureter devel- opment. FGF signaling in the ureteric epithelium augments Shh expression, which in turn activates the SHH-FOXF1-BMP4 signaling pathway in the ureteric mesenchyme, leading to mesenchymal and epithelial proliferation and differentiation. IM, inner mesenchyme; OM, outer mesenchyme; SMC, smooth muscle cell. (Adapted with permis- sion from Meuser M, Deuper L, Rudat C, et al. FGFR2 signaling enhanc- es the SHH-BMP4 signaling axis in early ureter development. Develop- ment. 2022;149:dev200021.)
