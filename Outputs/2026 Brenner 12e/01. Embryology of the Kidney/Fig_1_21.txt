Fig. 1.21 In vitro reconstitution of higher-order kidney structures. Nephron progenitors (NP) and stromal progenitors (SP) are either isolat- ed from E11.5 embryonic kidney or selectively induced from pluripotent stem cells (PSC). The obtained cells are further purified by flow cytom- etry sorting and counted. 25,000 NP cells and 50,000 SP cells are seeded into 96-well low cell binding plates and centrifuged to form sheetlike structures. Isolated embryonic ureteric bud (UB) or PSC-derived UB is placed in the center of this cell sheet, allowing the spontaneous formation of spheroids overnight. The reassembled spheroids can then differentiate into self-organized embryonic kidney structures including branched collecting ducts, peripheral nephron progenitors, and differentiated epithelial nephrons within a week in the organ culture setup.
