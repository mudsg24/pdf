Fig. 28.1 One-year survival comparing early goal- directed therapy (EGDT) to usual care in patients with sepsis Kaplan-Meier survival curves compar- ing EGDT to usual care in patients with sepsis from pooled patient-level data from the ProCESS, ARISE, and ProMISe trials. Kidney replacement therapy was required in 11.0% of patients who were randomized to EGDT as compared with 10.6% of patients randomized to usual care (OR: 1.02; 95% CI: 0.81–1.28; P = 0.88). (From The PRISIM Investigators. Goal-directed therapy for septic shock—a patient-level meta-analysis. N Engl J Med. 2017;376:2223–2234.)
