Fig. 71.5 Crossed fused ectopia. Renal ectopia is classified as simple (nonfused), fused, and bilateral. A fused kidney migrates across the midline and fuses to the lower pole of the normally positioned contralateral kidney. A simple crossed (nonfused) kidney migrates across the mid- line, does not fuse with the normally positioned contralateral kidney, and is usually positioned at the rim of the pelvis. In bilateral ectopia, both kidneys are ectopic and cross the midline with their native ureters, which are inserted normally into the bladder.
