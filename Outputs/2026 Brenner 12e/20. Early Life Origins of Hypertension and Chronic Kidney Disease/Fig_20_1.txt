(B) Potential in utero growth trajectories reflecting the presence or absence of growth restriction and timing of birth, which impact weight for gestational age. Low birth weight per se is no longer used as a risk threshold. Instead, appropriateness of weight for gestational age provides more granular identification of programming risk in infants born preterm or at term.
