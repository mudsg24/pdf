Fig. 20.13 (A) Cumulative incidence (%) of chronic kidney disease (CKD) according to number of birth-related risk factors (birth weight <10th percentile, birth weight for gestational age <10th percentile, and preterm birth before 37 weeks’ gestation). (B) Cumulative inci- dence of end-stage kidney failure (ESKF) according to number of birth-related risk factors (listed above). Compared with having none of the exposures, individuals with one exposure had a significantly higher risk of CKD but not ESKF. Individuals with two exposures had a higher risk than individuals with one exposure, and individuals with three exposures had the highest risk for both CKD and ESKF.
