Fig. 2.23 Transmission electron micrograph of the proximal convo- luted tubule from a normal human kidney. The mitochondria (M) are elongated and tortuous, occasion- ally doubling back on themselves. G, Golgi apparatus; IS, intercellular space; L, lysosome; Mv, microvilli forming the brush border; TL, tubule lumen.
