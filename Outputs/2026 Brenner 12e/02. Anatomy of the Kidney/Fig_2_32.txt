Fig. 2.32 Transmission electron mi- crograph of thin limbs of the loop of Henle in the initial inner medulla of rat kidney. Type III epithelium (arrows) has prominent apical plasma mem- brane microprojections. It is a low, flat epithelium with relatively few basolat- eral plasma membrane infoldings com- pared with type II epithelium. A small portion of type IV thin limb epithelium is also visible, which is also flat but has numerous tight junctions (arrowheads) due to abundant lateral interdigita- tions. (From Sands JM, Verlander JW. Functional anatomy of the kidney. In: McQueen C, ed. Comprehensive Toxi- cology. 3rd ed. St Louis: Elsevier; 2017.)
