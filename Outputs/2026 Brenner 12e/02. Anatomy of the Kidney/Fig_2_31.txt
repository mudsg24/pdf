Fig. 2.31 Transmission electron mi- crograph of type II epithelium of the thin limb of loop of Henle in the inner stripe of the outer medulla of a rat kidney. Compared with other thin limb types, type II epithelium is taller and has more organelles, prominent apical plasma membrane microprojections, and complex basolateral plasma mem- brane infoldings. The cells have exten- sive lateral interdigitations attached near the apical surface by short tight junctions (arrows). (Modified from Ver- lander JW.
