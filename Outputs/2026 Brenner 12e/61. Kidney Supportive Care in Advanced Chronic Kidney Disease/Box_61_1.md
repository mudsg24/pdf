Box 61.1 Situations in Which it is Appropriate to Consider Withdrawal From Dialysis<sup>1,20</sup> 1. Patients with decision-making capacity who, being fully in- formed and making voluntary choices, refuse dialysis or re- quest that dialysis be discontinued. 2. Patients who no longer possess decision-making capacity who have previously indicated refusal of dialysis through ap- propriate advance care planning. 3. Patients who no longer possess decision-making capacity and whose properly appointed legal agents/surrogates re- fuse dialysis or request that it be discontinued. 4. Patients with irreversible, profound neurologic impairment such that they lack signs of thought, sensation, purposefu behavior, and awareness of self and environment.
