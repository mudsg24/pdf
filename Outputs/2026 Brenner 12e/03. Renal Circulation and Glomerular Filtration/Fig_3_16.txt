Fig. 3.16 Electron micrographs (by D.A. Maddox) of a proximal tubule of a Munich-Wistar rat. Tubule was perfusion fixed with 1.25% glutaraldehyde, thereby also fixing red cells in adjacent capillaries. (A) The apposition of the basolateral surface of the tubular cells with the adja- cent peritubular capillaries is close, leaving little interstitial space where the two come in contact. (≈ ×13,000.) (B) The proximal tubule basement membrane (PC-BM) is relatively thick in comparison with the peritubular capillary endothelial basement membrane (PC-BM.) (≈×25,000.)
