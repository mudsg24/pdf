Fig. 62.21 Intradialysis urea kinetics: origin of Kt/V. Left, The nonlinear decrease in blood urea nitrogen during dialysis (solid line) becomes a straight line when plotted on a log scale (dashed line). The fractional rate of decrease is a constant, k = K/V, where k is the elimination constant, K is the clearance, and V is the urea distribution volume. Right, The solution to the equation describing first-order ki- netics shows that delivered Kt/V primarily depends on the predialysis and postdialysis blood urea nitrogen values (see text and legend for Fig. 62.20 for definition of variables shown). This oversimplified equa- tion is expanded in Fig. 62.20 to include the other important variables.
