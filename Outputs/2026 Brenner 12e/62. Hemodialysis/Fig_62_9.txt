Fig. 62.9 Angiography of a cephalic arch stenosis (white arrow) that developed in a patient with a left upper arm AV fistula. The high blood flow rate in a location where the vein turns 90 degrees leads to turbulent flow, leading to intimal hyperplasia. Lesions like this need repeated angioplasty and sometimes benefit from stent place- ment or use of drug-eluting balloons.
