Fig. 62.18 Effect of sequestration on serum phosphate levels and removal during dialysis. Measurements of serum inorganic phospho- rus concentrations were taken at 15-minute intervals during both high-flux and standard hemodialysis. The rapid flux of phosphorus due to it relatively high mass transfer-area coefficient caused levels to fall into the hypophosphatemic range (below the lower dotted line) during most of the 4-hour dialysis. A marked rebound continued for 4 hours after dialysis ended. (Modified from DeSoi CA, Umans JG. Phosphate kinetics during high-flux hemodialysis. J Am Soc Nephrol. 1993;4:1214−1218.)
