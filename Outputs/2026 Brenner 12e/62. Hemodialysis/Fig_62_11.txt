Fig. 62.11 Angiography of stenosis in the outflow vein of a left upper arm AV graft (gray arrow). This typical lesion in patients with an AV graft is felt due to turbulent flow as blood moves from graft to vein. These areas of intimal hyperplasia generally respond to angio- plasty but tend to recur, oftentimes requiring stenting or drug-eluting balloons to maintain long-term patency. There are also numerous in- tragraft stenoses. (Photo courtesy Dr. Suresh Appasamy, Interventiona Nephrology, University of California, Davis.)
