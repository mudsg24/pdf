Fig. 62.23 Double-pool model of urea mass balance in a hemo- dialysis patient. Addition of a second compartment to the diffusion model of urea mass balance shown in Fig. 62.22 accounts for the postdialysis rebound in urea concentration shown in Fig. 62.17 and, in general, is considered a more accurate model. K is the coefficient of mass transfer between compartments, analogous to dialyzer K A. Solution of the differential equation requires numeric analysis and is not commonly applied in dialysis clinics.
