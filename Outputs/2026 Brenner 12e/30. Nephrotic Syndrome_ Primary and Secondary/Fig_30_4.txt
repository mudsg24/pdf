Fig. 30.4 Graphic depiction of frequencies of different forms of glomerular disease identified in kidney biopsy specimens from patients with proteinuria of more than 3 g of protein/day evalu- ated at the University of North Carolina Nephropathology Labo- ratory. Some diseases that cause proteinuria are underrepresented because they are not always evaluated by kidney biopsy. For exam- ple, in many patients, steroid-responsive proteinuria is given a pre- sumptive diagnosis of minimal change disease and patients do not undergo biopsy, and most patients with diabetes and proteinuria are presumed to have diabetic glomerulosclerosis and do not undergo biopsy. GN, Glomerulonephritis.
