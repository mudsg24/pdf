Box 30.1 Alterations of Plasma Protein and Procoagulant Factors in Nephrotic Syndrome Plasma globulins Coagulation components – Decreased immunoglobulin G (IgG) – Increased factors II, V, VII, VIII, X, XIII – Normal or increased IgA, IgM, or IgE – Decreased factors IX, XI, XII – Increased α2- and β-globulins – Increased plasma fibrinogen – Decreased α1-globulins – Increased intravascular fibrin formation Metal- and hormone-binding proteins – Decreased fibrinolytic activity – Loss of metal binding proteins: iron, copper, zinc – Decreased plasma plasminogen – Loss of erythropoietin, transferrin – Increased tissue type plasminogen activator Loss of thyroid-binding globulin, vitamin D-binding protein, sex-hormone binding globulin, transcortin – Increased α2-macroglobulin – Decreased α1-antitrypsin Complement system – Decreased antithrombin III – Decreased factor B and I – Decreased protein S – Decreased C1q, C2, C8, C9 – Increased C3 Other procoagulant factors – Normal C1s, C4, and C1 inhibitor – Increased blood viscosity – Hemoconcentration – Thrombocytosis – Increased platelet aggregability
