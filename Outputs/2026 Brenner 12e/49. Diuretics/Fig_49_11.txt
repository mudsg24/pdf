Fig. 49.11 A diagrammatic depiction of mechanisms that may reduce glomerular capillary pressure and reversal of glomerular hy- perfiltration by a sodium-glucose–linked transport type 2 inhibitors (SGLT2is), angiotensin-converting enzyme inhibitors (ACEis) and angiotensin receptor blockers (ARBs). SGLT2i reduces reabsorption of sodium and chloride in the proximal tubule and increases their delivery and reabsorption at the macula densa segment. This activates the tubuloglomerular feedback (TGF) response to increase the resistance of the afferent arteriole and reduce the hydrostatic pressure in the glomerular capillaries. ACEis and ARBs lead to a greater reduction in resistance of the efferent than the afferent arteriole and reduce the hydrostatic pressure in glomerular capillaries by a different mechanism.
