Fig. 40.10 Pathophysiologic mechanisms of hyperoxaluria in inflammatory bowel disease or following bariatric surgery. Fatty acids and bile salts precipitate luminal calcium, which can be compounded by low dietary intake. Low luminal calcium frees up and increases unbound oxalate. Lack of Oxalobacter formigenes because of excess bile salts also contributes to high luminal oxalate levels. GI, Gastrointestinal.
