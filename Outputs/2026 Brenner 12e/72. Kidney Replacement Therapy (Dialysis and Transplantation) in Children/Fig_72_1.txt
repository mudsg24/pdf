Fig. 72.1 Severe renal osteodystrophy in a boy with end-stage renal disease with resolution obtained with medical treatment. (A) Age 1 year: severe rickets, absorption of the metaphyseal edges of the radius and ulna. Delayed bone age (6 months). (B) Age 2 years: deformation in the radius and ulna bones, widening of the metaphyseal edges, healing rickets. (C) Age 4 years: normal bone structure. Bone age is 3 1/2 years. (D) Age 2 years: deformation with angulations of the femur, tibia, and fibula bones—healing phase. (E) Age 4 years: marked improvement, although some angulation can still be seen.
