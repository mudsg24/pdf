Fig. 67.14 Aneurysm repair. (A) Aneurysm formation presented with wall thinning and bleeding. (B) The aneurysm is marked and an incision was made. (C) Using a pneumatic tourniquet, the aneurysmal tissue was removed. (D) Closing the incision with nonabsorbable sutures. (E) Closing the skin incision. (F) Appearance of the fistula after repair. (From Wang S, Wang MS. Successful use of partial aneurysmectomy and repair approach for managing complications of arteriovenous fistulas and grafts. J Vasc Surg. 2017;66[2]:545–553.)
