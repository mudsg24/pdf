Fig. 6.20 Maximal expression of the amiloride-sensitive epithelial Na<sup>+</sup> channel (ENaC) at the plasma membrane requires the coex pression of all three subunits (α-, β-, and γ-ENaC). (A) Amiloride-sensitive current in Xenopus oocytes expressing the individual subunits and various combinations thereof. (B) Surface expression is markedly enhanced in Xenopus oocytes that coexpress all three subunits. The individual cDNAs were engineered with an external epitope tag; expression of the channel proteins at the cell surface is measured by binding of a mono- clonal antibody (M Ab*) to the tag.
