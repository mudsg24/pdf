Fig. 6.11 Biphasic effect of AngII on Na<sup>+</sup> reabsorption in micro- perfused proximal tubules (PTs). The steady-state transepithelial Na<sup>+</sup> concentration gradient (peritubular-luminal), ΔC<sub>Na</sub>, that devel- oped in a stationary split droplet is used as an indication of the rate of active Na<sup>+</sup> reabsorption. This is plotted as a function of peritubu- lar AngII concentration; low concentrations activate Na<sup>+</sup> absorption by the PT, whereas higher concentrations inhibit it. (From Harris PJ, Navar LG. Tubular transport responses to angiotensin. Am J Physiol. 1985;248:F621–F630.)
