Fig. 6.4 Freeze-fracture electron microscopy images of tight junctions in mouse proximal and distal nephron. (A) Proximal con- voluted tubule, a “leaky” epithelium; the tight junction contains only one junctional strand, seen as a groove in the fracture face (arrows). (B) Distal convoluted tubule, a “tight” epithelium. The tight junction is deeper and contains several anastamosing strands, seen as grooves in the fracture face. (From Claude P, Goodenough DA. Fracture fac- es of zonulae occludentes from “tight” and “leaky” epithelia. J Cell Biol. 1973;58:390–400.)
