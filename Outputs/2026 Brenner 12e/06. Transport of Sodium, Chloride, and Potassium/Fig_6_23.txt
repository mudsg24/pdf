Fig. 6.23 K<sup>+</sup> transport along the nephron. Approximately 90% of filtered K<sup>+</sup> is reabsorbed by the PT and the loop of Henle. K<sup>+</sup> is se- creted along the initial and cortical collecting ducts. Net reabsorption occurs in response to K<sup>+</sup> depletion, primarily within the medullary col- lecting duct. ADH, Antidiuretic hormone; ALDO, aldosterone; CCD, cortical collecting duct; DCT, distal convoluted tubule; MCD, med- ullary collecting duct; PCT, proximal convoluted tubule; R, reabsorp- tion; S, secretion; TAL, thick ascending limb.
