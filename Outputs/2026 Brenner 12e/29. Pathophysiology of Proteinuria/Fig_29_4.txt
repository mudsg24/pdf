Fig. 29.4 Theoretical calculation of albumin concentration along the proximal tubule. Predictions of bulk albumin concentration (Cb) vs. axial position (z) in rats for Vmax ranging from 0.001 to 0.2 ng s<sup>–1</sup> mm<sup>–2</sup>. The curve that most closely corresponds to normal rats is that for Vmax = 0.086 ng s<sup>–1</sup> mm<sup>–2</sup>. (With permission from Lazzara MJ, Deen WM.
