Fig. e24.47 Poorly differentiated renal cell carcinoma, stage IV. Coronal (A) and axial (B) T2-weighted images show a heterogeneous mass in the lower pole of the left kidney with infiltration of the perinephric fat and extensive retroperitoneal lymphadenopathy. T1-weighted image (C) shows the masses to be intermediate in signal intensity. Gadolinium enhancement of axial T1-weighted images (D and E) make the local invasion and adenopathy more conspicuous and show that the left renal vein is encased, not invaded (arrows)
