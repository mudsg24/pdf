Fig. e24.8 Computed tomographic urogram: normal findings. The maximum-intensity projection (MIP) image (A) and the volume-rendered image (B) demonstrate the calyces, renal pelvis, ureters, and bladder. The MIP image is a slab, 15-mm thick, in the coronal plane. The volume rendered image was taken as the extraneous tissues adjacent to the kidneys were removed, and it highlights the genitourinary tract.
