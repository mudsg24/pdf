Fig. e24.46 Renal cell carcinoma with pseudocapsule, stage I. (A) T2-weighted image shows a heterogeneous, bright mass on the left with a well-defined pseudocapsule. (B) T1-weighted image confirms a well-defined dark mass involving the left renal cortex. (C to E) Axial gadolinium- enhanced T1-weighted images in the arterial, venous, and excretory phases demonstrate heterogeneous enhancement and no evidence of rena vein involvement. No perinephric invasion was found at surgery.
