Fig. 24.27 Autosomal dominant polycystic kidney disease. Axial (A) and coronal (C) T2-weighted images show bilateral renal cortical atro- phy and multiple cysts, most of which are bright. Axial T1-weighted image (B) shows multiple bright and dark structures that are not enhanced after gadolinium injection; this appearance was confirmed with subtraction image (D) and is therefore consistent with cysts.
