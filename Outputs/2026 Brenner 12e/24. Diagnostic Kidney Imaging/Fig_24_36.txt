Fig. 24.36 Renal artery stenosis: computed tomographic angiography (CTA). Axial (A) and coronal (B) slab maximum-intensity projection images demonstrate the atherosclerotic stenosis of the proximal renal artery. Note the accessory renal artery arising adjacent to the left main renal artery. Volume rendering of the CTA produced a three-dimensional display (C), which may be rotated for best viewing and analysis.
