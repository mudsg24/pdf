Fig. e24.44 Metastatic clear cell renal cell carcinoma, stage IV. T2-weighted (A) and gadolinium-enhanced T1-weighted (B) axial images show a large, heterogeneous mass with invasion of the adjacent liver and peritoneal metastases (arrowheads). (C and D) Coronal gadolinium- enhanced T1-weighted images show the large mass extending inferiorly and medially, with invasion of the inferior vena cava to the level of the hepatic veins (arrowheads).
