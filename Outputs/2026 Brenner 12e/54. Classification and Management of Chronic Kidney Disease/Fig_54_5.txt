Fig. 54.5 Results of a pooled analysis of the FIDELIO-DKD and FIGARO-DKD clinical trials of finerenone versus placebo in people with diabetic kidney disease. The analysis included 13,026 persons with a median follow-up of 3 years and showed that treatment with finerenone was associated with a 23% reduction in the cumulative incidence of the composite kidney outcome of time to kidney failure, sustained 57% or more decrease in eGFR from baseline, or kidney death. (From Bakris GL, Ruilope LM, Anker SD, et al. A prespecified exploratory analysis from FIDELITY examined finerenone use and kidney outcomes in patients with chronic kidney disease and type 2 diabetes. Kidney Int. 2023;103:196–206.)
