Fig. 14.9 Plasma arginine vasopressin (AVP) responses to ar- terial hypotension produced by the infusion of trimethaphan in patients with AVP-D (central diabetes insipidus) and osmore- ceptor dysfunction (adipsic AVP-D). Normal responses in healthy volunteers are shown by the shaded area. Note that despite absent or markedly blunted AVP responses to hyperosmolality, patients with osmoreceptor dysfunction respond normally to baroreceptor stimula- tion induced by hypotension. (From Baylis PH, Thompson CJ. Diabetes insipidus and hyperosmolar syndromes. In Becker KL, ed. Principles and Practice of Endocrinology and Metabolism. Philadelphia: JB Lippincott; 1995:257.)
