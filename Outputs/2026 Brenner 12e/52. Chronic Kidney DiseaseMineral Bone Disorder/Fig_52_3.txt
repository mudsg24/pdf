Fig. 52.3 Calcium-sensing receptor (CaR). Activation of the CaR by calcium stimulates phospholipase C, leading to increased inositol 1,4,5-triphosphate (IP ), which mobilizes intracellular calcium and in- hibits parathyroid hormone (PTH) synthesis. A decrease in serum cal- cium (Ca<sup>2+</sup>) inhibits intracellular signaling, leading to increased PTH synthesis and secretion. ER, Endoplasmic reticulum. (From Friedman PA, Goodman WG. PTH(1-84)/PTH(7-84): a balance of power. Am J Physiol Renal Physiol. 2006;290:F975–F984.)
