| Table 45.10 Diagnostic Criteria of ARPKD ARPKD Diagnosis Typical ARPKD renal findings on abdominal imaging+ 1 or more of the following:    Imaging consistent with biliary duct ectasia    Clinical/laboratory signs of congenital hepatic fibrosis leading to portal hypertension, indicated by hepatosplenomegaly and/or esophageal varices    Hepatobiliary pathology demonstrating a characteristic developmental biliary ductal plate abnormality resulting in congestive heart failure    Absence of renal enlargement or characteristic imaging findings in both parents, as confirmed by high-resolution ultrasonography (HRUS) examination    Pathologic (biopsy or autopsy) or genetic diagnosis of ARPKD in an affected sibling |
