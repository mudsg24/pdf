Fig. 41.1 Worldwide prevalence of diabe- tes.<sup>22</sup> The worldwide prevalence of diabetes was estimated at 537 million people in the year 2021. Approximately 40% of people with type 2 diabetes (95% of all diabetes cases) and 30% of those with type 1 diabetes (5% of all diabetes cases) develop diabetic kidney dis- ease. Half of all cases of CKD are attributable to diabetes. Most people with diabetic kidney disease (90%) die, mainly of heart failure or ath- erosclerotic cardiovascular events, with only a minority (10%) surviving to reach kidney failure.
