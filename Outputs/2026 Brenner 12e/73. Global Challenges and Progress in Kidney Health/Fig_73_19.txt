Fig. 73.19 Accessibility to kidney replacement therapy (KRT) by International Society of Nephrology (ISN) regions and World Bank Groups, pre and post pandemic. Accessibility was assessed as >50% of national population of kidney failure patients able to access hemodi alysis (HD), able to start peritoneal dialysis (PD), and able to receive kidney transplantation (KT) in 2019 and 2013. Trends presented in percent change from 2019. NIS, Newly independent states; OSEA, Oceania and Southeast Asia; PMP, per million population.
