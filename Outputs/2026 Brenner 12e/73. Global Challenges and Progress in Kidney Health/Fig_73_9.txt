Fig. 73.9 Chronic kidney disease (CKD) all-ages and age-standardized mortality rates. Age standardization accounts for the differences in the regional age structure and was made using the world 2019 GBD population. CKD metrics consider only the direct cause of death and do not include CVD mortality due to impaired kidney function. “North Africa” category reflects data for North Africa and Middle East.
