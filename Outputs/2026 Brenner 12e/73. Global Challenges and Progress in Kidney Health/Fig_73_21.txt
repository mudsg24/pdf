Fig. 73.21 Barriers to care in end-stage kidney disease in sub-Saharan Africa. Flow diagram showing barriers in access to dialysis con tributing to mortality in end-stage kidney failure (ESKF) in many sub-Saharan African countries. Green arrows show factors present to facilitate diagnosis of ESKF or referral for or access to dialysis; red arrows show absence of these factors. Most barriers are related to access to care, access to diagnosis, out-of-pocket payments needed, and infrastructural resources.<sup>61</sup> (From Olowu WA, Niang A, Osafo C, et al. Outcomes o acute kidney injury in children and adults in sub-Saharan Africa: a systematic review. Lancet Glob Health. 2016;4:e242–e250.
