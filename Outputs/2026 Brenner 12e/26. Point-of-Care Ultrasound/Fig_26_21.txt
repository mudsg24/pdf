Fig. 26.21 Normal central venous pressure (CVP) waveform creates flow pulsatility in the central veins. Hepatic vein Doppler displays a pulsatile flow characterized by one retrograde wave (a wave) and two antegrade waves (s and d waves). Given normal venous compliance, this pulsatility is attenuated so that normal portal and intrarenal venous flow is only minimally pulsatile. HVD, hepatic vein Doppler; IRVD, intrarena venous Doppler; PVD, portal vein Doppler.
