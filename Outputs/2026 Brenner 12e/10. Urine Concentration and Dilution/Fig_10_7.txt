Fig. 10.7 Typical osmolalities (in mOsm/kg H O) found in various vascular (left) and renal tubule (right) sites in rat kidneys. Fluid in the proximal tubule is always isosmotic with plasma (290 mOsm/kg H O). Fluid emerging from the loop of Henle (entering the early distal tubule) is always hypotonic. Osmolality in the late distal tubule increas- es to plasma level only during antidiuresis. Final urine is hypertonic when the circulating vasopressin level is high and hypotonic when the vasopressin level is low. A high osmolality is always maintained in the loop of Henle and vasa recta. During antidiuresis, osmolalities in all inner medullary structures are nearly equal. Osmolalities are some- what attenuated in the loop and vasa recta during water diuresis (not shown). AVP, Vasopressin; IMCD, inner medullary collecting duct.
