Fig. 10.15 Pathways of urea recycling in renal medulla. Solid blue lines represent a short-looped nephron (left) and a long-looped nephron (right). Transfer of urea between nephron segments is in- dicated by dashed red arrows labeled a, b, and c corresponding to recycling pathways described in the text. CD, Collecting duct; DCT, distal convoluted tubule; DL, descending limb; PST, proximal straight tubule; tAL, thin ascending limb; TAL, thick ascending limb; vr, vasa recta. (Knepper MA, Roch-Ramel F. Pathways of urea transport in the mammalian kidney. Kidney Int. 1987;31:629–633.)
