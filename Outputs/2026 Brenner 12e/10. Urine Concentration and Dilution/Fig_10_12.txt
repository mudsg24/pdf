Fig. 10.12 Urea permeabilities of mammalian renal tubule seg- ments. The width of each segment in the diagram is distorted to be pro- portional to the urea permeability of that segment. Numbers in parenthe- ses are measured values for the per- meability coefficient (×10<sup>−5</sup> cm/sec). Values are from isolated perfused tubule studies. ATL, Ascending thin limb; CTAL, cortical thick ascending limb; IMCD , initial inner medullary collecting duct; IMCD , terminal inner medullary collecting duct; LDL , thin descending limb of long-looped nephron in outer medulla; MTAL, medullary thick ascending limb; OMCD, outer medullary collecting duct; PST, proximal straight tubule; SDL, thin descending limb of short- looped nephron.
