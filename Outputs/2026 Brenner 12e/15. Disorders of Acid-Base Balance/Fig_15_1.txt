Fig. 15.1 HCO <sup>−</sup> reabsorption along the nephron. The proximal tubule reabsorbs approximately 80% of the filtered HCO <sup>−</sup>. Substantia amounts are also reabsorbed in the thick ascending limb. The remaining amounts (∼5%) are reabsorbed in the collecting duct, which is critical for final regulation of acid excretion. The mechanisms are described in Chapter 9, but the main apical transporters (Na-H exchange and H<sup>+</sup>-ATPase) are illustrated. Little HCO <sup>−</sup> (∼0%) remains in the final urine.
