Fig. 15.7 Cell models of ammonia synthesis and excretion pathways. (A) Proximal convoluted tubule. Ammonia is derived from glutamine precursors to produce two NH <sup>+</sup> and two HCO <sup>−</sup> molecules through an enzymatic pathway activated by acidemia and hypokalemia and inhibited by alkalemia and hyperkalemia. (B) Type A intercalated cell in collecting tubule. Ammonium entry across basolateral membrane through sub- stitution of NH <sup>+</sup> for K<sup>+</sup> on K<sup>+</sup> conductance and secreted across apical membrane via ROMK or RhCG (see text). In both A and B, NH diffusion coupled with H<sup>+</sup> secretion traps NH <sup>+</sup> in the tubule lumen.
