| Table 38: Final grade for overall quality of evidence A: High quality of evidence. We are confident that the true effect lies close to that of the estimate of the effect.    B: Moderate quality of evidence. The true effect is likely to be close to the estimate of the effect, but there is a possibility that it is substantially different.    C: Low quality of evidence. The true effect may be substantially different from the estimate of the effect.    D: Very low quality of evidence. The estimate of effect is very uncertain, and often will be far from the truth. |
