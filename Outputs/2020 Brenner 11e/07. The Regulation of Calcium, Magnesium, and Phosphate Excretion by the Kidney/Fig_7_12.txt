Fig. 7.12 Mechanism by which magnesium is transported in distal tubule cells. The majority of magnesium is reabsorbed by transcellular mechanisms. Mg2+ enters apically via the TRPM6/7 heteromeric channel, driven by the membrane voltage set by the K+ channel, Kv1.1. Its exit pathway is unknown but may be via CNNM2 or SLC41A1, perhaps acting as a basolateral Na-Mg exchanger. Active extrusion is likely driven by the Nat gradient generated by the Na-K-ATPase alpha subunit and accessory subunit, FXYD2. The basolateral K+ channel Kir 4.1 recycles K+ that enters via the Na-K-ATPase. See text for role of other regulators. EGF, Epidermal growth factor; EGFR, epidermal growth factor receptor.
