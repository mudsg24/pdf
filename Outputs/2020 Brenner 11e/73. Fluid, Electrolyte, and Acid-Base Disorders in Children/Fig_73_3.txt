Fig. 73.3 Schematic representation of a cell of the distal convoluted tubule (DCT) showing the transport systems involved in the reabsorption of NaCl. The thiazide-sensitive Na+-Cl cotransporter (NCC), the magnesium-transporting channel TRPM6, and the epithelial calcium channel (ECaC) are depicted on the luminal membrane of the cell. The Na+-K+-ATPase and the chloride channel CIC-Kb with their Barttin subunit are represented on the basolateral side. NCC, CIC-Kb, and TRPM6 are expressed in the early downstream segment of the DCT, and ECaC is expressed in the late downstream portion of DCT. TRPM6, Transient receptor potential channel melastatin 6.
