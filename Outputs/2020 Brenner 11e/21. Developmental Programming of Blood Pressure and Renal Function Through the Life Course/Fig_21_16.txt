Fig. 21.16 Correlation between kidney weight (Kw)-to-recipient weight (Rw) ratio and long-term graft survival in renal transplantation. A Kaplan-Meier survival curve shows the percentage of graft survival over time post-transplantation (in years). The group with a kidney weight to recipient weight ratio (Kw/Rw) of less than 2.3 g/kg^-3 shows a significantly steeper decline in graft survival compared to the group with a ratio greater than or equal to 2.3 g/kg^-3 (P = .016). (From Giral M, Foucher Y, Karam G, et al. Kidney and recipient weight incompatibility reduces long-term graft survival. J Am Soc Nephrol. 2010;21:1022-1029.)
