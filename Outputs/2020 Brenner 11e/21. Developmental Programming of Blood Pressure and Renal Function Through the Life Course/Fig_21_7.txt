Fig. 21.7 Influence of glomerular number on adaptation to diabetes in rats. (A) A scatterplot shows the relationship between mean glomerular number and birth weight. There appears to be a general positive correlation, with higher birth weights associated with higher glomerular numbers. (B) A plot of glomerular volume in rats on low-protein diet (solid lines) and normal-protein diet (dotted lines) is shown for control, diabetic, and insulin-treated diabetic groups. In the low-protein diet group, diabetic rats had a significantly larger glomerular volume compared to controls and insulin-treated rats (*P = .015*). (From Jones SE, Bilous RW, Flyvbjerg A, et al. Intra-uterine environment influences glomerular number and the acute renal adaptation to experimental diabetes. Diabetologia 2001;44:721-728.)
