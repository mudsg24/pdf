Fig. 21.9 (A) Nephron number, and (B) glomerular volume in Caucasian subjects with primary hypertension compared with controls. (A) A line graph shows the number of glomeruli for individual patients with hypertension and their matched controls. Patients with hypertension consistently have a lower number of glomeruli (mean 702,379) compared to matched controls (mean 1,429,200). (B) A line graph shows the volume of glomeruli for the same subjects. Patients with hypertension consistently have a larger glomerular volume (mean 6.50 x 10^-3 mm^3) compared to matched controls (mean 2.79 x 10^-3 mm^3). (From Keller G, Zimmer G, Mall G, et al. Nephron number in patients with primary hypertension. N Engl J Med. 2003;348:101-108.)
