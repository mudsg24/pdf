Fig. 21.12 Relationship between nephron number (number of glomeruli) and plasma retinol concentration in term rat fetuses. A scatter plot shows a strong positive correlation between plasma retinol concentration (µg/dL) and the number of glomeruli in term rat fetuses. As plasma retinol increases, the number of glomeruli increases (P < .001; R = 0.829). There are two groups shown: Vitamin A-deficient and Controls. (From Merlet-Benichou C. Influence of fetal environment on kidney development. Int J Dev Biol. 1999;43:453-456.)
