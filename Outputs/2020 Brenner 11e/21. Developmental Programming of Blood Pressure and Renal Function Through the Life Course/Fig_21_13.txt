Fig. 21.13 Effects of maternal hyperglycemia on nephron number in rat offspring. A scatter plot shows the nephron number for individual rat offspring from control and hyperglycemic (HG) groups. The mean value for the control group is represented by a dotted line. Offspring from the HG group generally have a lower nephron number compared to the controls. (From Amri K, Freund N, Vilar J, et al. Adverse effects of hyperglycemia on kidney development in rats: in vivo and in vitro studies. Diabetes 1999;48:2240-2245.)
