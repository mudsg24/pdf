Fig. 19.5 Distribution of kidney measures in NHANES participants with chronic kidney disease (CKD) older than 60 years and those with diabetes mellitus (DM), hypertension (HTN), self-reported cardiovascular disease (SR CVD), obesity (BMI > 30), and overall.
