Fig. 72.15 Long-term survival of 283 patients with childhood-onset end-stage kidney disease. Green dotted line, Survival rate considering all causes of death. Red line, Survival rate considering cardiovascular and cerebrovascular causes of death only. Blue line, Survival rate in the general population. Inset, Computed tomography sections from a 27-year-old male hemodialysis patient with extensive calcification in all three coronary arteries and aorta. (From Oh J, Wunsch R, Turzer M, et al. Advanced coronary and carotid arteriopathy in young adults with childhood-onset chronic renal failure. Circulation. 2002;106:100-105.) (A survival curve graph showing decreased survival rates over time for patients with childhood-onset ESRD. Survival is lower when considering all causes of death compared to only cardio/cerebrovascular causes, and both are significantly lower than the general population's survival rate.)
