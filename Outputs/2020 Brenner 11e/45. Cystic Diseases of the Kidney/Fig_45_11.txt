Fig. 45.11 Computed tomography (CT) of polycystic kidneys in a male patient whose serum creatinine level is within the normal range. (A) CT scan without contrast shows a radiopaque stone in the pelvis of the right kidney (arrow). (B) CT scan after intravenous administration of an iodinated radiocontrast agent. The stone now is obscured by contrast medium in the renal pelvis.
