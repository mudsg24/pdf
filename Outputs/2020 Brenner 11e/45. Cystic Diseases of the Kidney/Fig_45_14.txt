Fig. 45.14 Computed tomography (CT) scan of polycystic liver and kidneys in a female patient with autosomal dominant polycystic kidney disease. The serum creatinine level and liver function test results were within the normal range. An oral contrast agent was given to highlight the intestine, but no intravenous contrast was used. (A) Massive enlargement of the liver caused by intraparenchymal cysts. (B) CT scan at a lower level in the abdomen shows cystic kidneys and the lower portion of the cystic liver.
