Fig. 45.24 Medullary sponge kidney diagnosed by computed tomography (CT) scan. (A) Noncontrast axial image at the level of the kidneys demonstrates medullary calcinosis (arrows). (B) Coronal maximum intensity projection during the excretory phase of contrast-enhanced CT urography shows a medullary cyst (thick arrow) and dilated collecting tubules showing a papillary brush pattern in both kidneys (thin arrows). (Reprinted with permission from Koraishy FM, Ngo TT, Israel GM, Dahl NK. CT urography for the diagnosis of medullary sponge kidney. Am J Nephrol. 2014;39:165-170.)
