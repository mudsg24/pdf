Fig. 45.26 Acquired renal cystic disease. (A) Computed tomography (CT) scan with intravenous contrast. This man had renal failure caused by diabetic nephropathy and had received hemodialysis for 6 years before this examination. There is bilateral renal enlargement with diffuse cysts in the cortex and medulla. A solid tissue tumor (white dot) is seen in the anterior part of the left kidney. (B) CT scan of the original kidneys in a patient with a functioning renal allograft. Note the marked atrophy of the renal parenchyma in contrast to the cystic changes seen in (A).
