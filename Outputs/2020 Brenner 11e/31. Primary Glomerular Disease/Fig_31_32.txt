Fig. 31.32 Algorithm for the pathologic categorization of glomerular diseases with patterned or organized deposits. The first division is into amyloid versus nonamyloid disease and the second is into diseases caused by immunoglobulin (Ig) molecule deposition and those that are not. By the approach illustrated, fibrillary glomerulonephritis is distinguished from immunotactoid glomerulopathy based on the ultrastructural characteristics of the deposits. (Flowchart showing the categorization of glomerular diseases with organized deposits.)
