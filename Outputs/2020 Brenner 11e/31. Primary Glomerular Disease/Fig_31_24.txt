Fig. 31.24 Diagram of the ultrastructural features of acute poststrep tococcal glomerulonephritis. Note the subepithelial humplike dense deposits (straight arrow), subendothelial deposits (curved arrow), and mesangial deposits. There is endocapillary hypercellularity caused by neutrophil infiltration and endothelial and mesangial proliferation. (Courtesy JC Jennette.)
