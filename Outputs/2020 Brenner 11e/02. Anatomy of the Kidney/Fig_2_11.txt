Fig. 2.11 Transmission electron micrograph of normal rat glomerular capillary wall fixed in a 1% glutaraldehyde solution containing tannic acid. Note the relationship among the three layers of the glomerular basement membrane and the presence of the pedicels (P) embedded in the lamina rara externa (arrowhead). The filtration slit diaphragm with the central dense spot (thin arrow) is especially evident between the individual pedicels. The fenestrated endothelial lining of the capillary loop is shown below the basement membrane. A portion of an erythrocyte is located in the extreme lower right corner. BS, Bowman's space; CL, capillary lumen.
