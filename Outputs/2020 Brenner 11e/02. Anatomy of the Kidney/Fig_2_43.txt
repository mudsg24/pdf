Fig. 2.43 Transmission electron micrograph from rat cortical collecting duct illustrating type A (right) and type B (left) intercalated cells under basal conditions. Note differences in the density of the cytoplasm the location of the nuclei, the distribution of the mitochondria and cytoplasmic vesicles, and the number of apical projections between the two cell types. (Modified from Madsen KM, Verlander JW, Tisher CC. Relationship between structure and function in distal tubule and collecting duct. J Electron Microsc Tech. 1988;9:187-208.)
