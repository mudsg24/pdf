Fig. 2.23 Transmission electron micrograph of the proximal convoluted tubule from a normal human kidney. The mitochondria (M) are elongated and tortuous, occasionally doubling back on themselves. The endocytic apparatus, composed of apical vacuoles (AV), apical vesicles (V), and apical dense tubules (arrows), is well developed. G, Golgi apparatus; IS, intercellular space; L, lysosome; Mv, microvilli forming the brush border; TL, tubule lumen.
