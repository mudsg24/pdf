Fig. 2.25 Transmission electron micrographs illustrating the appearance of different types of lysosomes from human proximal tubules. (A) Lysosomes. Several mitochondria (M) are also shown. (B) Early stage of formation of an autophagic vacuole. (C) Fully formed autolysosome containing a mitochondrion undergoing digestion. (D) Autolysosome containing a microbody undergoing digestion. A multivesicular body (arrow) is also shown. (From Tisher CC, Bulger RE, Trump BF. Human renal ultrastructure. I: Proximal tubule of healthy individuals. Lab Invest. 1966;15:1357-1394.)
