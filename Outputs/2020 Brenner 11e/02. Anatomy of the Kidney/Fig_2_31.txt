Fig. 2.31 Transmission electron micrograph of type II epithelium of the thin limb of loop of Henle in the inner stripe of the outer medulla of a rat kidney. Compared with other thin limb types, type II epithelium is taller and has more organelles, prominent apical plasma membrane microprojections, and complex basolateral plasma membrane infoldings. The cells have extensive lateral interdigitations attached near the apical surface by short tight junctions (arrows). (Modified from Verlander JW. Normal ultrastructure of the kidney and lower urinary tract. Toxicol Pathol. 1998;Jan-Feb;26(1):1-17.)
