Fig. 59.1 Current classification system and nomenclature proposed by the Kidney Disease Improving Global Outcomes (KDIGO) in 2012. Chronic kidney disease (CKD) is defined as abnormalities of kidney structure or function, present for 3 months, with implications for health. CKD is classified based on cause, glomerular filtration rate (GFR) category, and albuminuria category. (From Kidney Disease: Improving Global Outcomes CKD Work Group. KDIGO 2012 clinical practice guideline for the evaluation and management of chronic kidney disease. Kidney Int. 2013;3(Suppl):1-150.
