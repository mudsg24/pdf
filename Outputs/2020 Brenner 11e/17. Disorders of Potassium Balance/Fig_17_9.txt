Fig. 17.9 Diagnostic algorithm in patients with primary hyperaldosteronism. Adrenal adenoma (APA) must be distinguished from glucocorticoid remediable hyperaldosteronism (FH-I [or GRA]), primary or unilateral adrenal hyperplasia (PAH), and idiopathic hyperaldosteronism (IHA). This requires computed axial tomography (CT), adrenal venous sampling (AVS), and the relevant diagnostic biochemical and hormonal assays (see text). (From Young WF Jr. Adrenalectomy for primary aldosteronism. Ann Intern Med. 2003:138(2):157-159.)
