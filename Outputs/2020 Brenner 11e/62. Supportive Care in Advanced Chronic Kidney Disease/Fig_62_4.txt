Fig. 62.4 Algorithm for the management of restlessness, agitation, and delirium at the end of life for patients with advanced chronic kidney disease (CKD). This flowchart details the management of restlessness, agitation, delirium, or hallucinations at the end of life. It begins by assessing if the patient is experiencing these symptoms. If yes, it suggests considering reversible causes. If symptoms persist, the first-line treatment is Haloperidol. The algorithm notes that Haloperidol can accumulate in ESKD and the dose should be reduced. If agitation or restlessness continues, Haloperidol should be discontinued and Methotrimeprazine started. The dose of Methotrimeprazine can be increased if the patient requires more than 3 PRN doses in 24 hours. For severe delirium, sedation with Midazolam is considered. If assistance with symptom management is required, an urgent palliative care referral is appropriate. ATC, Around the clock; BID, twice a day; ESKD, end-stage kidney disease; PO, per os; PRN, as needed; q1h, every 1 hour; q6h, every 6 hours; q8h, every 8 hours; SC, subcutaneous.
