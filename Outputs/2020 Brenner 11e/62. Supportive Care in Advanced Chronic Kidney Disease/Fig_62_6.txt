Fig. 62.6 An integrated approach to the care of patients who have chosen conservative kidney management. This diagram shows a four-step process for conservative kidney management. Step 1 (Plan) is "Choosing conservative kidney management." Step 2 (Plan) is "Initiate care planning." Step 3 (Manage) is a cyclical process involving "Conservative kidney management" (clinical assessments, symptom management, CKD management), "Advance care planning," "Establish community support and referrals," "Crisis management plan," "End-of-life plan," and "Update conservative kidney management care plan." Step 4 (Support) is "Grief & loss." CKM, Conservative kidney management. (Reproduced with permission from Dr. Sara Davison, Kidney Supportive Care Research Group.)
