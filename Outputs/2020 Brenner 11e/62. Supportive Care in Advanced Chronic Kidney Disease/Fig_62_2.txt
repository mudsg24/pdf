Fig. 62.2 An approach to kidney supportive care. This flowchart illustrates an approach to kidney supportive care. It begins with "Identification of patients most likely to benefit from supportive care," which involves assessing "Patients at high risk for death within the next year" using the "Surprise Question." It then lists "General indicators of decline" (e.g., functional decline, repeated admissions), "Specific clinical indicators" (e.g., poor tolerance of dialysis, high symptom burden), and "Decision making" issues (e.g., difficulty determining goals of care). These assessments feed into the "Key components of kidney supportive care," which include "Symptom management and relief of suffering," "Prognostication," and "Advance care planning." All these components are underpinned by "Culturally competent shared decision" making and lead towards "Death" and subsequent "Bereavement support." CKM, Conservative kidney management; QOL, quality of life. (Adapted from Davison SN. Integrating palliative care for patients with advanced chronic kidney disease: recent advances, remaining challenges. J Palliat Care 2011;27:53-61.)
