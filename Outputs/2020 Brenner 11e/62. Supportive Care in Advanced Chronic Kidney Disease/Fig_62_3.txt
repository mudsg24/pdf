Fig. 62.3 Algorithm for the management of pain at the end of life for patients with advanced chronic kidney disease. This is a flowchart for pain management at the end of life. It starts with "Does the patient have pain?". If yes, it advises non-pharmacological interventions like repositioning and reassurance. It then asks if the patient is already on an opioid. The algorithm branches based on whether pain is incident (related to specific activities) or around the clock. For incident pain, fentanyl is recommended. For around-the-clock pain, low-dose hydromorphone is suggested. The flowchart provides specific dosages for oral (PO) and subcutaneous (SC) routes and advises converting to subcutaneous route if oral intake is not possible. It includes a strong note that morphine is not recommended for patients with kidney impairment. If pain is difficult to control, a referral to a palliative care specialist is recommended. ATC, Around the clock; PO, per os; q1h, every 1 hour; q4h, every 4 hours; SC, subcutaneous; SL, sublingual. (Adapted with permission from Sara N. Davison, Kidney Supportive Care Research Group. Conservative Kidney Management Care Pathway. 2016. Available from: www.ckmcare.com. Last accessed September 20, 2017.)
