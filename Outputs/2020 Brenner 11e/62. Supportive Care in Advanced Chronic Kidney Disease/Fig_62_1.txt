Fig. 62.1 Conceptual framework for kidney supportive care. A graph shows two overlapping curves over a timeline from "Presentation of illness" to "Death". The first curve, labeled "Curative/remittive care," starts high and gradually decreases. The second curve, "Kidney supportive care," starts low and gradually increases, eventually encompassing a final phase labeled "Terminal/end-of-life care." After death, a period of "Bereavement" is shown. Below the timeline, text explains that care is "Based on supportive care needs rather than prognosis" until a "Patient is identified as dying (usually with a prognosis ≤ 6 months)."
