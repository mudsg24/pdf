Fig. 62.5 A framework for facilitating advanced care planning (ACP) in patients with advanced chronic kidney disease. This is a detailed flowchart for facilitating ACP. It starts with "Identification of patients most likely to benefit from ACP." The process involves several stages: "Introduce ACP" (normalizing the topic), "Determine patient and family readiness," "Patient-led ACP" (respecting patient preferences), "Facilitation of ACP: Key elements" (exploring health condition, values, goals, and past experiences), "Decision making" (determining goals of care, identifying a substitute decision-maker), and finally "Documentation and development of a follow-up plan" (creating advance directives, physician orders like POLST). CKM, Conservative kidney management. (Adapted from Davison SN. Facilitating advance care planning for patients with end-stage renal disease: the patient perspective. Clin J Am Soc Nephrol. 2006;1:1023-1028; Davison SN, Torgunrud C. The creation of an advance care planning process for patients with ESRD. Am J Kidney Dis. 2007;49:27-36.)
