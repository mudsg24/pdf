Fig. 68.15 Interventions to Promote AV Fistula Maturation: Cumulative survival, defined from the time of access cannulation to permanent failure, is shorter in patients receiving two or more interventions before arteriovenous fistula maturation compared with those with zero interventions (HR, 2.07; 95% CI, 1.21 to 2.94; P = 0.0001). (Reproduced from Lee T, Ullah A, Allon M, et al: Decreased Cumulative Access Survival in Arteriovenous Fistulas Requiring Interventions to Promote Maturation.
