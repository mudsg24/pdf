Fig. 61.4 Although the average steady-state concentrations (Cave) are identical regardless of which dosage adjustment strategy one decides to use, the concentration-time profile will be markedly different if one changes the dose and maintains the dosing interval (t) constant (Scenario A), versus changing the dosing interval and maintaining the dose constant (Scenario B), or changing both (Scenario C). An image of a graph showing three different drug concentration profiles over time for three dosing scenarios (A, B, C). All three scenarios result in the same average steady-state concentration (Cave), indicated by a dashed line. Scenario A shows frequent, small peaks and troughs. Scenario B shows infrequent, very high peaks and very low troughs. Scenario C is intermediate between A and B. A table indicates the dose, interval (T), Cmax, Cmin, and Cave for each scenario.
