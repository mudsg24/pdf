Table 70.20 Precautions for Procedures and Surgery in Kidney Transplant Recipients * Caution with radiocontrast exposure * Maintain hydration * Avoid nephrotoxic antibiotics and analgesic * “Stress-dose” steroids is not always necessary * If enteral route of medication is contraindicated, give CNI IV or sublingual * Monitor allograft function, plasma potassium and acid-base balance daily * Consider wound-healing impairment with sirolimus
