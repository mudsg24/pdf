Fig. 12.7 Schematic of principal cells in the aldosterone-sensitive distal nephron (ASDN). The ASDN includes the distal third of the distal convoluted tubule (DCT), connecting tubule, and collecting duct. The Na+-K+-adenosine triphosphatase (Na+-K+-ATPase) establishes the gradients for passive apical entry of sodium through the epithelial sodium channel (ENaC). Transport of sodium through the ENaC creates a negative lumen potential that drives potassium secretion into the lumen. Potassium is also recycled at the basolateral surface, which facilitates potassium exchange across the Na+-K+-ATPase. Chloride (Cl) moves via paracellular and transcellular pathways. There is evidence to support aldosterone (Aldo) actions in other segments, particularly the sodium-chloride cotransporter-expressing portions of the DCT (DCT1 and DCT2).
