Fig. 10.12 Localization of aquaporins in the outer medullary collecting duct (outer stripe) of rat kidney. Images show sections immunostained in (A) for aquaporin-4 (AQP4) (red) and (B) for aquaporin-2 (AQP2) (green). The merged image in (C) shows that AQP2 is largely apical in this region, but both AQP2 and AQP4 are present on basolateral membranes. Intercalated cells are not stained with either antibody and appear as darker gaps among the other cells. In (C), nuclei are stained with 4',6-diamidino-2-phenylindole (DAPI). Scale bar, 10 μm.
