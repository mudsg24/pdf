Fig. 50.10 Effects of dietary salt intake on the diuretic braking phenomenon. Renal Nat excretion (mmol/6 hours) for 24 hours before and after the first (F1) and third (F3) daily doses of furosemide (A and C; 40 mg intravenously) and (B) bumetanide (B1, B3, 1 mg intravenously) in groups of 8 to 10 normal subjects equilibrated to fixed daily Nat intakes. The average level of Nat intake (mmol/6 hours) is shown by broken horizontal lines. Negative Nat balance is indicated by blue bars and positive Nat balance by light green areas. The mean ± standard error of the mean (SEM) values for diuretic-induced increases in Nat excretion above baseline values (AUNAV) for 6 hours after the administration of the diuretic are shown at the top. (Modified from Wilcox CS, Mitch WE, Kelly RA, et al. Response of the kidney to furosemide. J Lab Clin Med. 1983;102:450-458.)
