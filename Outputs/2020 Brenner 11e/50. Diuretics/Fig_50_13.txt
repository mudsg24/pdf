Fig. 50.13 Diagrammatic representation of an approach to the management of a patient with resistance to a loop diuretic. CD, Collecting duct diuretic (e.g., amiloride, triamterene, or spironolactone); DCT, distal convoluted tubule diuretic (e.g., thiazide); NSAIDs, nonsteroidal antiinflammatory drugs; PT, proximal tubule diuretic (e.g., acetazolamide). (From Ellison DH, Wilcox CS. Diuretics: use in edema and the problem of resistance. In Brady HR, Wilcox CS, eds. Therapy in Nephrology and Hypertension. ed 2. London: Elsevier Science; 2003.)
