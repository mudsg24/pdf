Fig. 25.71 Renal cell carcinoma: computed tomographic scan. Contrast material-enhanced axial image in the corticomedullary phase. Note the heterogeneously enhanced mass in the anterior aspect of the left kidney. This is a stage II renal cell carcinoma, inasmuch as it has extended through the renal capsule into Gerota's fascia. This proved to be a renal cell carcinoma, clear cell type.
