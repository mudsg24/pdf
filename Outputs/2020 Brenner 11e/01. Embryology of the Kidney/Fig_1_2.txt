Fig. 1.2 Three stages of mammalian kidney development. The pronephros and mesonephros develop in a rostral-to-caudal direction and the tubules are aligned adjacent to the wolffian or nephric duct (WD). The metanephros develops from an outgrowth of the distal end of the wolffian duct known as the ureteric bud epithelium (UB) and a cluster of cells known as the metanephric mesenchyme (MM). The pronephros and mesonephros are vestigial structures in mice and humans and are regressed by the time the metanephros is well developed.
