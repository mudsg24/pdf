Fig. 1.11 Cre-lox homologous gene recombination system. Simpli-fied overview of Cre recombinase-mediated homologous recombination for generation of tissue-specific conditional null mutations of a target gene. Cre recombinase expression is engineered to be driven under the control of tissue specific promoter (promoters). The target gene (or typically certain exons within the target chromosomal locus) are flanked with loxP sites (recognition sites for Cre recombinase). Cre activity in specific cell types mediates the excision of the loxP-flanked (floxed) target gene, creating a null allele.
