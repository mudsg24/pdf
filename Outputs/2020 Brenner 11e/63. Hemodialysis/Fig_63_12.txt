Fig. 63.12 Flow-limited clearance. A logarithmic decline in solute concentrations, indicated by the arrows on either side of the membrane, is depicted from the dialyzer inlet at the left to the blood outlet on the right. This predictable decline is attributable to relatively rapid diffusion of solute across the membrane and forms the basis for Eq. 5. Flux is equal to the product of membrane-solute KA and the log mean gradient. Solute flux and removal are maximized by countercurrent flow of blood and dialysate.
