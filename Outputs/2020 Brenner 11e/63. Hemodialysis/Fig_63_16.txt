Fig. 63.16 Equilibrated postdialysis blood urea nitrogen (BUN), the basis for eKt/V. Precise measurements of BUN every 15 minutes during a typical patient's 2.5-hour hemodialysis shows a logarithmic decrease in concentration during the treatment and a rapid rebound that is complete approximately 1 hour later. The double-pool model shown in Fig. 63.21 and the solid line in the graph predict the concentra- tions accurately. The equilibrated postdialysis BUN is an extrapolated value shown as the large solid circle.
