Fig. 6.10 Biphasic effect of angiotensin II (Ang II) on Na+ reabsorption in microperfused proximal tubules. The steady-state transepithelial Nat concentration gradient (peritubular-luminal), ACNa, that developed in a stationary split droplet is used as an indication of the rate of active Na+ reabsorption. This is plotted as a function of peritubular Ang II concentration; low concentrations activate Nat absorption by the proximal tubule, whereas higher concentrations inhibit it. (From Harris PJ, Navar LG. Tubular transport responses to angiotensin. Am J Physiol. 1985;248:F621-F630)
