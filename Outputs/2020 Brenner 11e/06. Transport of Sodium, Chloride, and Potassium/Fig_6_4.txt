Fig. 6.4 Freeze-fracture electron microscopy images of tight junctions in mouse proximal and distal nephron. (A) Proximal convoluted tubule, a "leaky" epithelium; the tight junction contains only one junctional strand, seen as a groove in the fracture face (arrows). (B) Distal convoluted tubule, a "tight" epithelium. The tight junction is deeper and contains several anastamosing strands, seen as grooves in the fracture face. (From Claude P, Goodenough DA. Fracture faces of zonulae occludentes from "tight" and "leaky" epithelia. J Cell Biol. 1973;58: 390-400.)
