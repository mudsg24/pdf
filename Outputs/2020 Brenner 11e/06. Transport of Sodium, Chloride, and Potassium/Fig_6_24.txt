Fig. 6.24 Schematic representation of medullary K+ recycling. Medullary interstitial K+ increases considerably after dietary K+ loading due to the combined effects of secretion in the cortical collecting duct, absorption in the outer medullary collecting duct, thick ascending limb, and inner medullary collecting duct, and secretion in the descending thin limb. See text for details. (From Stokes JB. Consequences of potassium recycling in the renal medulla. Effects of ion transport by the medullary thick ascending limb of the loop of Henle. J Clin Invest. 1982;70:219-229.)
