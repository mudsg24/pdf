Fig. 39.20 Effect of blood pressure (BP) lowering in diabetes. Forest plot of standardized associations between 10-mm Hg lower systolic BP and all-cause mortality, macrovascular outcomes, and microvascular outcomes in a metaanalysis of clinical trials of BP lowering therapy that included patients with type 2 diabetes. The area of each square is proportional to the inverse variance of the estimate. Horizontal lines indicate 95% Cls of the estimate. (From Emdin CA, Rahimi K, Neal B, Callender T, Perkovic V, Patel A. Blood pressure lowering in type 2 diabetes: a systematic review and meta-analysis. JAMA. 2015;313(6):603-615. With permission.)
