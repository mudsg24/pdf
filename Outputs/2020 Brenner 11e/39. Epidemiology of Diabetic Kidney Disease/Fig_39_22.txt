Fig. 39.22 Kaplan-Meier plot of cumulative probabilities of acute kidney injury and hyperkalemia in patients with combined angiotensin inhibition in the VA Nephron-D trial. Acute kidney injury was defined as acute kidney injury requiring hospitalization or occurring during a hospitalization. Hyperkalemia was defined as a potassium level over 6.0 mmol per liter or that required an emergency room visit, hospitalization, or dialysis. The P values were calculated with the use of a stratified log-rank test.
