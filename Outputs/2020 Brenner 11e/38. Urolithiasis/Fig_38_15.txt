Fig. 38.15 Pathophysiologic mechanisms of hyperoxaluria. Oxalate balance is determined by ingestion and endogenous production versus intestinal and urinary excretion. Intestinal handling can be bidrectional, and luminal degradation is facilitated by microbial degradation. Urinary excretion is the net result of filtration minus tubular reabsorption. Hyperoxaluria can be caused by the following: (1) increased dietary ingestion; (2) increased gut absorption or decreased secretion; (3) increased endogenous hepatic production; (4) decreased intestinal bacterial metabolism; or (5) renal hyperexcretion.
