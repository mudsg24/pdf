Fig. 55.11 Factors involved in the increased risk of bleeding in patients with renal failure. Roman numerals with or without lower case letters indicate clotting factors; ADP, Adenosine diphosphate; AT, antithrombin; Ca2+, calcium ion; E, endothelium; GP, glycoprotein; NO, nitric oxide; PGI2, prostaglandin 12; T, thrombocyte; tPA, tissue-type plasminogen activator; V, vessel; vWF, von Willebrand factor. (From Lutz J, Menke J, Sollinger D, et al. Haemostasis in chronic kidney disease. Nephrol Dial Transpl. 2014;29:29-40.)
