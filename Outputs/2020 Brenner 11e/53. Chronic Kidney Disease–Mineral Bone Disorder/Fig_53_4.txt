Fig. 53.4 Normalization of serum calcium by multiple actions of parathyroid hormone (PTH). Serum levels of ionized calcium (Ca) are maintained in the normal range by induction of increases in the secretion of PTH. PTH acts to increase bone resorption, renal calcium reabsorption, and the conversion of 25(OH)D to calcitriol in the kidney, thereby increasing gastrointestinal calcium absorption. The blue boxes indicate processes that are abnormal in chronic kidney disease, leading to altered calcium homeostasis. PO4, Phosphate. (From Moe SM. Calcium, phosphorus, and vitamin D metabolism in renal disease and chronic renal failure. In: Kopple JD, Massry SG, eds. Nutritional Management of Renal Disease. Philadelphia: Lippincott Williams & Wilkins; 2004:261-285.)
