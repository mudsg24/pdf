Fig. 53.1 Intestinal phosphate transport. Approximately 50% of phosphate (Pi) transport is sodium (Na+) dependent, due to active transport, and regulated by a number of factors. The remaining phosphate transport is sodium independent and due to paracellular or transcellular transport. FGF-23, Fibroblast growth factor 23; MEPE, matrix extracellular phosphoglycoprotein; Na+/K+ ATPase, sodium-potassium adenosine triphosphatase; NaPi-llb, the sodium Pi cotransporter. (From Lee GJ, Marks J. Intestinal phosphate transport: a therapeutic target in chronic kidney disease and beyond? Pediatr Nephrol. 2015;30:363-371. With permission.)
