Fig. 30.5 Theoretical calculation of albumin concentration along the proximal tubule. Predictions of bulk albumin concentration (Cb) vs. axial position (z) in rats for Vmax ranging from 0.001 to 0.2 ng/sec per millimeter squared. The curve that most closely corresponds to normal rats is that for Vmax = 0.086 ng/sec per millimeter squared. (From Lazzara MJ, Deen WM. Model of albumin reabsorption in the proximal tubule. Am J Physiol Renal Physiol. 2007;292:F430-439, with permission.)
