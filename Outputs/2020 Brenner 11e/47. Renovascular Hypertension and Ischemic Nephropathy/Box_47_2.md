Box 47.2 Syndromes Associated With Renovascular Hypertension<sup>a</sup> 1. Early- or late-onset hypertension (<30 years or >50 years) 2. Acceleration of treated essential hypertension 3. Deterioration of renal function in treated essential hypertension 4. Acute renal failure during treatment of hypertension 5. Flash pulmonary edema 6. Progressive renal failure 7. Refractory congestive cardiac failure <sup>a</sup>These “syndromes” should alert the clinician to the possible contribution of renovascular disease in a given patient. Syndromes 5 to 7 are most common in patients with bilateral disease, many of whom are treated as “essential hypertension” until these characteristics appear.
