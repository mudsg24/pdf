Fig. 47.11 (A) Computed tomography angiogram of high-grade vascular occlusion leading to left kidney atrophy while the right kidney is well perfused beyond an endovascular stent. (B) T2 signals from magnetic resonance imaging (MRI) illustrate a gradient of perfusion from the cortex to lower levels in the deeper medullary sections, especially in the left kidney (dashed line depicts the level of axial sections shown in panels [C] and [D]). (C and D) Mapping of the blood oxygen level-dependent (BOLD) magnetic resonance R2* levels (a function of deoxyhemoglobin) illustrates the normal gradient in the right kidney from the cortex (low R2*, blue) to deeper medullary segments that have progressively higher levels of R2* (green to red). The R2* map of the left kidney (D) illustrates overt cortical hypoxia evident beyond critical vascular occlusion associated, with a larger fraction of the slice having high R2* values (red) and fractional medullary hypoxia.
