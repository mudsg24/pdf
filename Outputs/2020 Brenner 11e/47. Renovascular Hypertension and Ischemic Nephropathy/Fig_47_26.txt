Fig. 47.26 Computed tomography angiogram illustrating complex surgical repair of aortic and vascular perfusion to a solitary functioning kidney. There is a vascular graft from the right common iliac artery to the right renal artery (arrow). Although surgical reconstruction of the renal vessels is less commonly performed since the introduction of endovascular techniques, it provides an essential alternative for complex occlusive disease and failed endovascular stenting in selected patients.
