Box 29.8 Indications for Renal Replacement Therapy **Absolute Indications** *   Volume overload unresponsive to diuretic therapy *   Persistent hyperkalemia despite medical therapy *   Severe metabolic acidosis *   Overt uremic symptoms-encephalopathy, pericarditis, uremic bleeding diathesis **Relative Indications** *   Progressive azotemia without uremic manifestations *   Persistent oliguria
