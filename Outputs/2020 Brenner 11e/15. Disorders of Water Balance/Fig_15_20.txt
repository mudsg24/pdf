Fig. 15.20 Recommended goals (green) and limits (red) for correction of hyponatremia based on the risk of producing osmotic demyelination syndrome (ODS). Also shown are recommendations for relowering of the serum [Na] to goals for patients presenting with serum [Na⁺] < 120 mmol/L who exceed the recommended limits of correction in the first 24 hours. (From Verbalis JG, Goldsmith SR, Greenberg A, et al: Diagnosis, evaluation, and treatment of hyponatremia: expert panel recommendations. Am J Med. 2013;126(Suppl 1):S1-S42.)
