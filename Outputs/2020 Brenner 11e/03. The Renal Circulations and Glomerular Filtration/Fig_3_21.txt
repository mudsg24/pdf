Fig. 3.21 Interaction of endothelial cells with smooth muscle or mesangial cells. As indicated in the text, several agents interact with endothelial cells to produce the vasodilator nitric oxide (NO). Others yield vasoconstriction (see text and other sources). Angiotensin-converting enzyme converts angiotensin I to the potent vasoconstrictor angiotensin II. ATP-ADP, Adenosine trisphosphate-adenosine diphosphate; CGRP, calcitonin gene-related peptide; EDCF, endothelial-derived constrict- ing factor; EDHP, endothelial-derived hyperpolarizing factor; EET, epoxyeicosatrienoic acid; PGF2a, prostaglandin F20; PGI2, prostaglandin 12; TGF transforming growth factor beta; TXA2, thromboxane A2.
