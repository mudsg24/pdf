Fig. 3.14 Hydraulic and colloid osmotic pressure profiles along idealized glomerular capillaries in hydropenic and euvolemic rats. Values shown are mean values derived from the studies shown in Fig. 3.13. The transcapillary hydraulic pressure gradient, AP, is equal to PGC - PT, and the transcapillary colloid osmotic pressure gradient, Δπ, is equal to AGC - ΠBS, where PGC and PBS are the hydraulic pressures in the glomerular capillary and Bowman's space, respectively, and Gc and ABS are the corresponding colloid osmotic pressures. Because the value of ABS is negligible, An essentially equals AGC. PUF is the ultrafiltration pressure at any point. The area between the AP and Aπ curves represents the net ultrafiltration pressure, PUF. Left, Lines A and B represent two of the many possible profiles under conditions of filtration pressure equilibrium; line D represents disequilibrium and line C represents the hypothetic linear ∆π profile. Right, Pressure profile after correction for surgical induced loss of plasma volume depicts a small positive AP at the efferent level indicative of disequilibrium conditions. QA, Glomerular plasma flow; SNGFR, single-nephron glomerular filtration rate.
