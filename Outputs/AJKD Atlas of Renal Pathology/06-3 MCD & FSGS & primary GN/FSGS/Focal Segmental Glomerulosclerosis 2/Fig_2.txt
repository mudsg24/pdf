More advanced lesions of FSGS are present in this biopsy, with a central glomerulus showing global sclerosis, the glomerulus on the left showing well-deﬁned peripheral segmental sclerosis, and the glomerulus on the right showing no lesions in this section. There is moderate interstitial ﬁbrosis and tubular atrophy. The presence of globally sclerotic glomeruli does not aid in the speciﬁc diagnosis of FSGS, because global sclerosis may occur nonspeciﬁcally related to other injury processes or aging. (Jones’ silver stain, 3100).
